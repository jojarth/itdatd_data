## Countering and Dispelling

Magic can be undone. A spell can be stopped as it is cast, or ended after it has taken hold.

- **Countering** stops a spell while it is being cast. The spell fails, and the caster's Actions and spell slot are spent for nothing.
- **Dispelling** ends a spell that is already in effect.

### Opposed Spells

Some spells oppose one another. A spell and its reverse always oppose each other, such as Light and Darkness, or Haste and Slow. Other spells that oppose each other say so in their Casting entry, such as *Counters and dispels: Darkness*.

You can use an opposed spell to counter or dispel only a spell of **the same level or lower**. Your class's rules for reverse spells still apply: you can use a reverse form only if you could cast it.

#### Countering with an Opposed Spell

When you see or hear a creature casting a spell that one of your prepared spells opposes, you recognize it. You can cast your prepared spell as a **Master Reaction** to counter it, as long as the caster is within your spell's range. ⚑ *If your spell's range is Self or Touch, the caster must be within 30 feet.*

The counter is automatic. Neither spell takes effect: both are expended, and the countered caster's Actions and Master Action for the round are spent. You can counter a spell in the phase it is cast or, if it is channeled, in any phase while it is being channeled.

A counter is itself a spell being cast, so it can be countered in turn.

#### Dispelling with an Opposed Spell

When you cast a spell on a creature, object, or area that is under the effects of a spell it opposes, both spells end. Neither takes effect, and neither remains in play.

Opposed spells can never exist in the same place. You can cast Light near magical darkness, but if any part of the light would touch the darkness, both are dispelled.

### Counterspell and Dispel Magic

The spells *Counterspell* and *Dispel Magic*, and their greater forms, can stop or end any spell, magical or clerical, but they aren't certain. The caster of the targeted spell makes a **Saving Throw vs Spell**. On a success, their spell is unaffected.

The higher the targeted spell's level, the harder it is to stop: each spell says how its level changes the save.

**Absent casters.** A spell keeps the strength of the one who cast it. If its caster isn't present, or is long dead, the GM uses the save that caster would have made.

## Counterspell

**Level 3 · Magic-User**
**Casting:** 1 Master Reaction, when you see or hear a creature within range casting a spell
**Range:** 60 feet · **Duration:** Instant

**Spell Effect.** You tear the threads of a spell before they can be tied. The caster must make a Saving Throw vs Spell. They gain a +1 Situation bonus for each level their spell is above 3rd. On a failure, their spell is countered: it takes no effect, and their Actions, Master Action, and spell slot are spent. On a success, their spell is cast normally.

**Preparation.** Study the spell while tying a cord into a knot and then pulling it free, again and again, until it unties at a touch.
**Casting.** Snap your Casting Hand closed toward the caster, as if catching something thrown, and speak a single sharp word.

## Greater Counterspell

**Level 5 · Magic-User**
**Casting:** 1 Master Reaction, when you see or hear a creature within range casting a spell
**Range:** 120 feet · **Duration:** Instant

**Spell Effect.** As Counterspell, but the caster gains a +1 Situation bonus for each level their spell is above 5th, and suffers a −1 Situation penalty for each level it is below 5th. A cantrip counts as 0 level, so its caster suffers a −5 penalty.

**Counts as Counterspell** for [overlapping spells](#) and any rule that refers to Counterspell.

**Preparation.** Study the spell while unraveling a length of knotted cord, finishing before the last knot comes loose by itself. ⚑
**Casting.** Close your Casting Hand into a fist toward the caster and draw it back sharply, speaking a single word that cuts across theirs.

## Dispel Magic

**Level 3 · Cleric, Theurge**
**Casting:** 1 Master Action; Phase 4 ⚑
**Range:** 60 feet · **Duration:** Instant

**Spell Effect.** Choose one creature, object, or magical effect within range. ⚑ *Every spell affecting it must be tested.* For each spell, its caster makes a Saving Throw vs Spell, gaining a +1 Situation bonus for each level that spell is above 3rd. On a failure, the spell ends.

**Preparation.** Study the spell while washing a stained cloth in running water until it comes clean.
**Casting.** Sweep your Casting Hand across the target as if wiping a slate clean, while speaking a short, plain prayer of undoing.

## Greater Dispel Magic

**Level 5 · Cleric, Theurge**
**Casting:** 1 Master Action; Phase 4
**Range:** 120 feet · **Duration:** Instant

**Spell Effect.** Choose a point within range. ⚑ *Every spell affecting a creature, object, or magical effect within a 20-foot radius of that point must be tested.* For each spell, its caster makes a Saving Throw vs Spell, gaining a +1 Situation bonus for each level the spell is above 5th, and suffering a −1 Situation penalty for each level it is below 5th. On a failure, the spell ends. You can choose creatures and objects in the area to leave untouched.

**Counts as Dispel Magic** for overlapping spells and any rule that refers to Dispel Magic.

**Preparation.** Study the spell while washing a stained cloth in running water, then wringing it out over the earth.
**Casting.** Sweep your Casting Hand in a wide circle toward the chosen point while speaking the prayer of undoing in full.

### ⚑ Calls to confirm

1. **Self and Touch spells** counter at 30 feet.
2. **Dispel Magic tests every spell on its target**, not one of your choice. Players often don't know what's affecting a creature, and testing every spell avoids that. It also means dispelling an ally ends their blessings too, unless you use the Greater version's "leave untouched."
3. **Greater Dispel Magic** gets a 20-foot radius and lets you spare chosen creatures. Greater Counterspell gets only the range and the save penalty.
4. **Dispel Magic's casting phase** is a guess (Phase 4, like Haste).
5. **Spell components** are placeholders in your style; change freely.
6. **The class split.** Only Magic-Users can counter, and only Clerics and Theurges can dispel, except through opposed spells. This gives each a role. I think that's good, but confirm it's what you meant.
