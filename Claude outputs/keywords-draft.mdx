## Keywords
Keywords are used to define who and what you are. They describe your fundamental nature, ancestry, training, and the experiences that make your character unique.

Spells, magical items, and features often use keywords to determine whether you are a valid target, whether you can utilize them, or whether they are available to you as a character option.

Although many keywords consist of a single word, some may contain multiple words and are always treated as a single keyword, for example "Knight of the Black Lion" is a single keyword.

Keywords are divided into five categories: **Essence, Form, Species, Class,** and **Adventure**. There are light rules that govern each category, but during play your keywords can largely be treated as one list.

### Form Keywords
<p style="margin-top: 0;"><small><i>Your Body</i></small></p>

Form keywords describe the shape of a creature's body. Your ancestry determines your Form, and every ancestry in this book is **Humanoid**.

Other Forms include **Beast, Monstrosity, Plant, Amorphous, Swarm, Incorporeal,** and **Object**. You will mostly meet these on the creatures you face, and in the spells and features that target them.

### Species Keywords
<p style="margin-top: 0;"><small><i>Your Heritage</i></small></p>

Species keywords identify what kind of creature something is. Your ancestry determines which Species keywords you possess. If your heritage spans more than one people, you possess the Species keywords of each; a half-orc is both **Human** and **Orc**.

Creatures you encounter often have more than one Species keyword, such as a broad group and their own kind. A horse is both **Equine** and **Horse**, so a spell that affects **Equine** creatures affects it, as does a feature that names **Horse**.
