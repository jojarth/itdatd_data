## Fitted and Munition Armor

Most armor is **fitted**: made or adjusted to the measure of the one who wears it. **Munition armor** is made in quantity to a standard pattern, stored in armories, and issued to soldiers and levies. It costs far less than fitted armor of the same kind, but it is heavier and leaves more gaps.

Munition armor has 1 lower AC and 1 higher EP (both equipped and packed) than fitted armor of the same category. It otherwise counts as armor of that category for every rule, including proficiency. There is no munition Light Armor; padded and quilted defenses are already cheap to make to measure.
