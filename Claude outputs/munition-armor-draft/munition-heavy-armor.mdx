Munition Heavy Armor is a harness assembled from standard, interchangeable pieces kept in an armory. It covers most of the body as Heavy Armor does, but its pieces are thick, crudely finished, and seldom adjusted to the wearer, leaving it heavier and less protective than a fitted harness.

Munition Heavy Armor may take the form of a mail harness issued to a garrison, a brigandine with mismatched arms and legs, or a mail-and-plate harness gathered piece by piece from stores. It counts as Heavy Armor for all rules that refer to Heavy Armor.
