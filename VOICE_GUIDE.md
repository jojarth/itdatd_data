# ITD&TD Writing Voice

Use this guide for new and revised project writing. Match the kind of page: rulebook chapters explain play, while data entries stay brief and reference focused.

## Voice

- Write plainly and directly to the player or Game Master.
- Keep the tone conversational, practical, and grounded, with restrained flavor.
- Explain what to do and when, in the order the reader needs it.
- Use concrete examples when they make a rule or procedure easier to understand.
- Be frank about risk and character death while reinforcing that play is collaborative and everyone is there to have fun.
- Keep short equipment and feature descriptions concise; reserve longer explanations for rules chapters.

Follow the project's established game terms. Do not treat existing typos or inconsistent capitalization as part of the voice; those can be corrected separately.
