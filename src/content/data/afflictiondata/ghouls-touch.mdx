import ConditionLink from '../../../components/conditions/ConditionLink.astro';

**Stage 1 (1 round):** [**Action Delay.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#action-delay) You act 1 phase later than normal. **Stiff.** You suffer a -2 Condition penalty to Agility and Precision tests. Your joints begin to stiffen.

**Stage 2 (1 round):** [**Action Delay.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#action-delay) You act 2 phases later than normal. **Clumsy.** You suffer a -2 Condition penalty to attack rolls.

**Stage 3 (1 round):** [**Fewer Actions.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#fewer-actions) You have 1 fewer Action each round. [**Faltering.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#faltering) Your Master Actions cost 1 additional Action. [**Exposed.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed) Every foe holds Combat Supremacy against you. Your arms are locking up.

**Stage 4 (10 minutes):** You are <ConditionLink id="paralyzed" />.

**Lasting Effect:** If you reach Stage 4, you suffer 1 point of Agility drain when the poison ends.
