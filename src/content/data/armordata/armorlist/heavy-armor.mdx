Heavy armor is a complete and burdensome defensive ensemble, usually combining rigid or metal body armor with a helmet and additional protection for the limbs. It may take the form of a mail harness, lamellar harness, scale harness, brigandine, coat of plates, jack of plates, or mail-and-plate armor.
