Light Armor consists primarily of layered, quilted, stuffed, or tightly woven textile. It offers useful protection while remaining relatively flexible and easy to wear for long periods.

Light Armor may take the form of an aketon, gambeson, padded jack, quilted coat, layered linen defense, or another comparable textile garment. Minor reinforcements of hide, horn, cord, or metal may be present, but they do not form the armor's principal defense.
