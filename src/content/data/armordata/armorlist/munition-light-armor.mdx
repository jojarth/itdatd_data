Munition Light Armor is quilted or padded armor sewn in bulk to a few rough sizes. It is stuffed thick to make up for its loose fit, which leaves it bulky, hot, and slow to dry, and it protects less than a coat made to measure.

Munition Light Armor may take the form of an issue jack, a levy's padded coat, or a quilted doublet handed out from a town's stores. It counts as Light Armor for all rules that refer to Light Armor.

Munition Light Armor exists for those who must arm many people at once, such as a lord raising a peasant levy or an employer outfitting a band of low-level hirelings. For a single adventurer, fitted Light Armor is almost always the better purchase: it costs little more and weighs nothing while worn.
