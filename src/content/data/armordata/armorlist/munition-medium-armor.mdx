Munition Medium Armor is Medium Armor made in quantity rather than for a particular wearer. Its pieces are cut to a standard pattern from rougher iron, so the armor is heavier than it should be, sits poorly on the body, and leaves gaps a fitted coat would close.

Munition Medium Armor may take the form of an armory-issue haubergeon, a plain jack of plates, a rough scale coat, or a breastplate drawn from a lord's stores. It counts as Medium Armor for all rules that refer to Medium Armor.
