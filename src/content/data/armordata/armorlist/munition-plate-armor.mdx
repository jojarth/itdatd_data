Munition Plate Armor is plate made to a standard pattern for soldiers rather than to the measure of a single wearer. Its plates are thicker and plainer than a fitted harness, and it often leaves the lower legs or hands to mail, cloth, or nothing at all.

Munition Plate Armor may take the form of an almain rivet, a foot soldier's half-armor, or a cuirassier's harness drawn from an armory. It counts as Plate Armor for all rules that refer to Plate Armor.
