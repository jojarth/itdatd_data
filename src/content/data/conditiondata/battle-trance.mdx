**Battle Trance** is a condition in which a creature gives itself over to the fury of battle. A Battle Trance always comes from a source, such as a class feature or a spell. That source describes what the trance does for you, how long it lasts, and what happens when it ends.

Every Battle Trance also follows these rules:

* **One at a time.** You can be under only one Battle Trance at a time. If an effect would give you a new one, choose whether to keep your current trance and ignore the new one, or end your current trance and gain the new one. Ending a trance this way still applies its ending effects.
* **A clear head to begin.** You cannot enter a Battle Trance while you are <ConditionLink id="slow" /> or <ConditionLink id="sluggish" />. Becoming Slow or Sluggish while in one does not end it.
* **No room for subtle work.** While in a Battle Trance, you cannot cast spells, read spell scrolls, use magical rods, staves, or wands, or perform delicate tasks or tasks that require concentration.
* **Shared benefits.** Some effects grant another creature the benefits of your Battle Trance. That creature gains only the listed benefits. It is not under a Battle Trance, and these rules do not apply to it.
* **Burning out.** Starting with the round after you enter it, your Battle Trance ends at the end of any round in which you did not make a melee attack, take damage, or spend 1 Action to maintain it. Unless the trance's source says otherwise, the creature under the Battle Trance spends this Action. You can also end your Battle Trance at any time (no Action required).
