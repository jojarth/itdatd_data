A **Blinded** creature cannot see.

* **Sightless.** You automatically fail Attribute Tests that rely on sight, and you are immune to effects that require you to see them.
* [**Hindered.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#hindered) Your movement costs are doubled.
* [**Exposed.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed) Every foe holds Combat Supremacy against you.

If you are <ConditionLink id="dazzled" /> when you become Blinded, the Dazzled condition ends.
