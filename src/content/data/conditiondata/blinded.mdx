A **Blinded** creature cannot see.

<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Sightless">You automatically fail Attribute Tests that rely on sight, and you are immune to effects that require you to see them.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Hindered" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#hindered">Your movement costs are doubled.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Exposed" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed">Every foe holds Combat Supremacy against you.</EffectRow>
</EffectTable>

If you are <ConditionLink id="dazzled" /> when you become Blinded, the Dazzled condition ends.
