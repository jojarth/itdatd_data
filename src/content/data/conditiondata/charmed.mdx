A **Charmed** creature regards the creature that charmed it, and that creature's allies, as trusted friends.

<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Beguiled">You do not fully control your own behavior. You will not knowingly harm the creature that charmed you or its allies, and you may defend them.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Obedient">If you share a language, you will usually follow the commands of the creature that charmed you, as long as they seem reasonable and are not contrary to your habits or nature. You never obey a command that is suicidal or obviously harmful to you.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Duration">The effect that charms you sets how long the charm lasts and whether you can repeat your Saving Throw.</EffectRow>
</EffectTable>

**Breaking the Charm.** The charm ends if the creature that charmed you, or one of its allies, deliberately harms you in a way you recognize as an attack on you. Harm you could reasonably take as an accident, such as being caught at the edge of a blast aimed at someone else, does not break the charm. The GM decides what the Charmed creature believes.
