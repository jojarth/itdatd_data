A **Confused** creature's mind is scrambled.

* **Friendless.** You cannot treat any creature as an ally.
* [**Exposed.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed) Every foe holds Combat Supremacy against you.
* **Erratic.** You do not fully control what you do. The effect that makes you Confused normally describes how you behave, often with its own table. If it does not, roll a d6 at the start of each round and behave as follows for that round:

| **d6** | **Behavior This Round** |
| --- | --- |
| **1** | Wander in a random direction, using all of your movement. |
| **2–3** | Stand dazed and do nothing. |
| **4–5** | Attack the nearest creature, friend or foe. |
| **6** | Act normally. |
