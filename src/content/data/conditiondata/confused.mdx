A **Confused** creature's mind is scrambled.

<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Friendless">You cannot treat any creature as an ally.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Exposed" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed">Every foe holds Combat Supremacy against you.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Erratic">You do not fully control what you do. The effect that makes you Confused normally describes how you behave, often with its own table. If it does not, roll a d6 at the start of each round and behave as follows for that round:</EffectRow>
</EffectTable>

| **d6** | **Behavior This Round** |
| --- | --- |
| **1** | Wander in a random direction, using all of your movement. |
| **2–3** | Stand dazed and do nothing. |
| **4–5** | Attack the nearest creature, friend or foe. |
| **6** | Act normally. |
