A **Dazed** creature's head is ringing.

* [**Action Delay.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#action-delay) You act 1 phase later than normal.
* **Strained.** Any effect you maintain by spending Actions, such as a Battle Trance or a Spellsong, costs 1 additional Action to maintain.
