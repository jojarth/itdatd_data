A **Dazzled** creature's eyes are overwhelmed by bright light. It can still see, but only through glare and spots, and foes close at hand can slip past its guard. A brighter or longer exposure makes the condition last longer, not hit harder.

If you become <ConditionLink id="blinded" />, the Dazzled condition ends.
