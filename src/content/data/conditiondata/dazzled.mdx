A **Dazzled** creature's eyes are overwhelmed by bright light. It can still see, but only through glare and spots.

<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Glare">You suffer a -2 Condition penalty to attack rolls and Attribute Tests that rely on sight.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Exposed" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed">Foes adjacent to you hold Combat Supremacy against you.</EffectRow>
</EffectTable>

A brighter or longer exposure makes the condition last longer, not hit harder. If you become <ConditionLink id="blinded" />, the Dazzled condition ends.
