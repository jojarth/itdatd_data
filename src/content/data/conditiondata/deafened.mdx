A **Deafened** creature cannot hear.

* **Unhearing.** You automatically fail Attribute Tests that rely on hearing, and you are immune to effects that require you to hear them.
* **Unwary.** You automatically fail any roll or test to avoid being caught by Surprise, unless you start the fight.
