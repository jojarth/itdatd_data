<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Unhearing">You automatically fail Attribute Tests that rely on hearing, and you are immune to effects that require you to hear them.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Unwary">You automatically fail any roll or test to avoid being caught by Surprise, unless you start the fight.</EffectRow>
