An **Entangled** creature is caught in webs, a net, grasping vines, or tangled rope. Unlike a <ConditionLink id="restrained" /> creature, it can still move and fight, but only slowly and clumsily.

* [**Hindered.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#hindered) Your movement costs are doubled.
* **Clumsy.** You suffer a -1 Condition penalty to attack rolls.
* [**Faltering.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#faltering) Your Master Actions cost 1 additional Action, and Master Reactions cost 1 Action.

**Breaking Free.** The effect that entangles you describes how you can break free, such as an Attribute Test, cutting or burning the strands, or simply waiting for the effect to end.
