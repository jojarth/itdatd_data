**Exhausted (X)** represents deep fatigue from forced marches, hunger, thirst, cold, and other hardships. Unlike most conditions, Exhausted values add together: if you are Exhausted (1) and gain 2 more levels of exhaustion, you become Exhausted (3).

You suffer the effects listed for your current level and every level below it. When the same effect appears at more than one level, use the higher value.

| **Exhausted** | **Effects Gained** |
| --- | --- |
| **1** | -1 Condition penalty to physical Attribute Tests (Brawn, Heartiness, Agility, and Precision) |
| **2** | [**Reduced Speed.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#reduced-speed) Your speed is reduced by 5 feet. |
| **3** | -1 Condition penalty to all Attribute Tests, attack rolls, and Saving Throws |
| **4** | [**Action Delay.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#action-delay) You act 1 phase later than normal. |
| **5** | Your maximum Grit is halved (round down). If your current Grit is higher, it drops to match; when this ends, recover Grit normally. |
| **6** | The penalty from level 3 becomes -2 |
| **7** | [**Reduced Speed.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#reduced-speed) Your speed is reduced by 10 feet. [**Fewer Actions.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#fewer-actions) You have 1 fewer Action each round. |
| **8** | Your maximum Grit is reduced to one quarter (round down) instead of half |
| **9** | The penalty from level 3 becomes -3 |
| **10** | [**Action Delay.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#action-delay) You act 2 phases later than normal. [**Reduced Speed.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#reduced-speed) Your speed is reduced by 15 feet. |
| **11** | [**Fewer Actions.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#fewer-actions) You have 2 fewer Actions each round. |
| **12** | The penalty from level 3 becomes -4 |
| **13** | However hardy your body, you die of exhaustion. |

**Death from Exhaustion.** Your body can endure a number of levels of exhaustion equal to half your Heartiness score (rounded down) plus 2, to a minimum of 4. If your Exhausted value ever exceeds this limit, you die. For example, a character with a Heartiness of 15 can endure Exhausted (9), but dies at Exhausted (10). Regardless of Heartiness, every creature dies at Exhausted (13).

**Recovering.** Each Night's Rest in which you eat and drink enough reduces your Exhausted value by 1. Other effects that reduce exhaustion state how many levels they remove. If your Exhausted value reaches 0, the condition ends.
