A **Grappled** creature is in another creature's grip. The creature holding you is <ConditionLink id="grappling" />. The two of you are locked together until the grapple ends.

**Escaping.** You can spend 1 Action to try to break free, making the same opposed test as the Grapple maneuver. If you win, this condition ends.

**Ending.** This condition also ends if the creature holding you lets go, becomes Incapacitated, or is moved out of reach of you.
