A **Grappled** creature is in another creature's grip. The creature holding you is <ConditionLink id="grappling" />.

* **Held.** You cannot move on your own. Once per round, you or the creature holding you can make an opposed test to move: the creature trying to move tests Brawn, and the other tests Brawn or Agility, applying the Common Combat Maneuver Modifiers. The winner moves and drags the other along; the loser cannot move that round. No test is needed if the other creature cannot act.
* [**Hindered.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#hindered) Whoever moves has their movement costs doubled.
* **No Room for Spells.** You cannot cast spells, read spell scrolls, use magical rods, staves, or wands, or perform delicate tasks.
* **Close Quarters.** You can attack only the creature holding you, and only with a Brawling weapon or a weapon with the Light property.
* **Shield Hampered.** You cannot use Shield Block or benefit from your shield's Critical Protection, and its AC bonus is reduced by 1 (to a minimum of +0). Its bonus to Saving Throws vs Breath still applies.

**Escaping.** You can spend 1 Action to try to break free, making the same opposed test as the Grapple maneuver. If you win, this condition ends.

**Ending.** This condition also ends if the creature holding you lets go, can no longer act, or is moved out of reach of you.
