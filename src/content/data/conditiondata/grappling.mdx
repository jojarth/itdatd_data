A creature that is **Grappling** has hold of another creature, which is <ConditionLink id="grappled" />. One of your hands is occupied holding it.

* **Dragging.** You can move only by bringing the Grappled creature along, using the opposed test described on the Grappled condition.
* [**Hindered.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#hindered) Your movement costs are doubled while you drag it.
* **Close Quarters.** You can attack only the creature you are holding, and only with a Brawling weapon or a weapon with the Light property.

**Ending.** You can let go at any time (no Action required). This condition also ends when the other creature is no longer Grappled.
