A creature that is **Grappling** has hold of another creature, which is <ConditionLink id="grappled" />. One of your hands is occupied holding it for as long as this condition lasts.

**Moving.** You can move only by bringing the Grappled creature along. Once per round, either of you can make an opposed test to move, as described under the Held hindrance on the Grappled creature. The winner moves and drags the other along.

**Ending.** You can let go at any time (no Action required). This condition also ends when the other creature is no longer Grappled.
