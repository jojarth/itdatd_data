A **Hasted** creature moves and acts with unnatural speed.

<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Quick" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#action-delay">You act 2 phases earlier than normal, but never before Phase 1.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Extra Action" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#fewer-actions">You have 1 additional Action each round. This never grants additional Master Actions or Reactions.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Effortless" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#faltering">Your Master Actions cost 1 fewer Action, to a minimum of 1 Action.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Fleet">Your speed increases by 10 feet.</EffectRow>
</EffectTable>
