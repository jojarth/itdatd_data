A **Hasted** creature moves and acts with unnatural speed.

* [**Quick.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#action-delay) You act 2 phases earlier than normal, but never before Phase 1.
* [**Extra Action.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#fewer-actions) You have 1 additional Action each round. This never grants additional Master Actions or Reactions.
* [**Effortless.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#faltering) Your Master Actions cost 1 fewer Action, to a minimum of 1 Action.
* **Fleet.** Your speed increases by 10 feet.
