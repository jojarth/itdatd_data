An **Invisible** creature cannot be seen without the aid of magic or special senses.

* **Unseen.** You hold Combat Supremacy against any creature that cannot see you, and a creature that cannot see you cannot hold Combat Supremacy against you.

The effect that makes you Invisible states how long it lasts and what ends it.
