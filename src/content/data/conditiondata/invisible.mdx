An **Invisible** creature cannot be seen without the aid of magic or special senses.

<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Unseen">You hold Combat Supremacy against any creature that cannot see you, and a creature that cannot see you cannot hold Combat Supremacy against you.</EffectRow>
</EffectTable>

The effect that makes you Invisible states how long it lasts and what ends it.
