You are **Moderately Encumbered** while the weight you carry places you in that encumbrance category (see [Encumbrance](/playersguide/06-equipment/02-encumbrance/)). The condition ends as soon as your load drops below that category, such as when you drop your pack.

<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Reduced Speed" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#reduced-speed">Your speed is reduced by 5 feet.</EffectRow>
</EffectTable>

Your Move Speed can never be reduced below 5 feet.
