You are **Over Encumbered** while the weight you carry places you in that encumbrance category (see [Encumbrance](/playersguide/06-equipment/02-encumbrance/)). The condition ends as soon as your load drops below that category, such as when you drop your pack.

Your Move Speed becomes 5 feet, and you suffer a -3 Situation penalty to physical Attribute Tests that your load could reasonably interfere with.

**Encumbrance Penalties.** Unlike other conditions, this test penalty is a Situation penalty, so it stacks with Condition penalties such as those from being Exhausted. It applies to tests such as climbing, jumping, swimming, balancing, tumbling, crawling, or moving stealthily, but never to Attack Rolls or Damage Rolls. Your Move Speed can never be reduced below 5 feet by encumbrance or Move Penalties.
