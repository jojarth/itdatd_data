A **Petrified** creature is transformed, along with everything it is wearing and carrying, into a solid, inanimate substance, usually stone. While Petrified:

<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Stone">You cannot Act, React, move, or speak, and you are unaware of your surroundings.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Rigid">You automatically fail Brawn, Agility, and Precision tests, and Saving Throws vs Breath.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Exposed" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed">Every foe holds Combat Supremacy against you.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Heavy">Your weight increases tenfold, and you do not age.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Unliving">You automatically succeed on Saving Throws vs Doom.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Mindless">You are immune to psychic damage and to effects that affect the mind.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Hardened">You have resistance to all other damage.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Suspended">All other conditions and effects on you are suspended, not ended. Their durations do not pass while you are Petrified, and they resume when this condition ends.</EffectRow>
</EffectTable>

**Awake in Stone.** Some petrification, such as an ancient curse or the gaze of a particularly cruel creature, leaves the victim's mind awake and trapped within the stone. Such an effect says so. A creature petrified this way is not Unaware, is not immune to effects that affect the mind, and takes psychic damage normally (without resistance). It remains conscious for as long as it stays stone. The GM may call for rolls on the Mind lasting injury table as the years pass.
