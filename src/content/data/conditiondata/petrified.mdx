A **Petrified** creature is transformed, along with everything it is wearing and carrying, into a solid, inanimate substance, usually stone. While Petrified:

* Your weight increases tenfold, and you do not age.
* You automatically succeed on Saving Throws vs Doom.
* You are immune to effects that affect the mind.
* You have resistance to all damage.
* All other conditions and effects on you are suspended, not ended. Their durations do not pass while you are Petrified, and they resume when this condition ends.
