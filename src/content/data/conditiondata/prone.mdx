A **Prone** creature is lying on the ground.

* [**Crawling.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#hindered) Your movement costs are doubled.
* **Low Stance.** You suffer a -2 Condition penalty to attack rolls.
* [**Exposed.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed) Foes adjacent to you hold Combat Supremacy against you.
* **Low Profile.** Ranged attacks against you suffer a -2 Condition penalty.

**Standing Up.** You can end this condition by standing up. Standing up costs 1 Action and provokes Opportunity Attacks. Alternatively, you can rise carefully as a Master Action costing 1 Action, which does not provoke Opportunity Attacks.
