A **Prone** creature is lying on the ground.

<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Crawling" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#hindered">Your movement costs are doubled.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Low Stance">You suffer a -2 Condition penalty to attack rolls.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Exposed" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed">Foes adjacent to you hold Combat Supremacy against you.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Low Profile">Ranged attacks against you suffer a -2 Condition penalty.</EffectRow>
</EffectTable>

**Standing Up.** You can end this condition by standing up. Standing up costs 1 Action and provokes Opportunity Attacks. Alternatively, you can rise carefully as a Master Action costing 1 Action, which does not provoke Opportunity Attacks.
