A **Restrained** creature is bound, entangled, or held fast.

<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Pinned">You cannot move, and effects that would increase your Move Speed do nothing.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Bound">You suffer a -2 Condition penalty to Saving Throws vs Breath.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Exposed" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed">Every foe holds Combat Supremacy against you.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Overmatched">You cannot hold Combat Supremacy against any creature.</EffectRow>
</EffectTable>

**Breaking Free.** The effect that restrains you describes how you can break free.
