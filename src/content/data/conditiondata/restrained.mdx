A **Restrained** creature is bound, entangled, or held fast.

* **Pinned.** You cannot move, and effects that would increase your Move Speed do nothing.
* **Bound.** You suffer a -2 Condition penalty to Saving Throws vs Breath.
* [**Exposed.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed) Every foe holds Combat Supremacy against you.
* **Overmatched.** You cannot hold Combat Supremacy against any creature.

**Breaking Free.** The effect that restrains you describes how you can break free.
