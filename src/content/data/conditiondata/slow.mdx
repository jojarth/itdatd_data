A **Slow** creature moves and acts as if wading through deep water.

* [**Action Delay.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#action-delay) You act 2 phases later than normal.
* [**Faltering.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#faltering) Your Master Actions cost 1 additional Action, and Master Reactions cost 1 Action.
* [**Reduced Speed.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#reduced-speed) Your speed is reduced by 10 feet.
