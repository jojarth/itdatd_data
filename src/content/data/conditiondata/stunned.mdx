A **Stunned** creature is reeling from a heavy blow, a thunderclap, or a sudden shock. Its body is not yet beyond its control: it does not fall, and foes cannot simply finish it off.

<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Reeling">You cannot Act or React.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Off Balance">You automatically fail Saving Throws vs Breath.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Exposed" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed">Every foe holds Combat Supremacy against you.</EffectRow>
</EffectTable>

Unless its source says otherwise, being Stunned lasts until the end of the next round.
