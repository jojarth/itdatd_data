A **Stunned** creature is reeling from a heavy blow, a thunderclap, or a sudden shock. It cannot act and cannot guard itself, but its body is not yet beyond its control: it does not fall, and its foes cannot simply finish it off.

Unless its source says otherwise, being Stunned lasts until the end of the next round.
