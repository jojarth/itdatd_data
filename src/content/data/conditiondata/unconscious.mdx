An **Unconscious** creature is senseless and helpless. When you become Unconscious, you drop whatever you are holding and become <ConditionLink id="prone" />. Prone is a separate condition: you are still Prone when you wake.

* **Senseless.** You cannot Act, React, move, or speak, and you are unaware of your surroundings.
* **Limp.** You automatically fail Brawn, Agility, and Precision tests, and Saving Throws vs Breath.
* [**Exposed.**](/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed) Every foe holds Combat Supremacy against you.
* **Overmatched.** You cannot hold Combat Supremacy against any creature.
* **Defenseless.** A foe adjacent to you deals its weapon attack damage directly to your Hit Points instead of your Grit, and you cannot choose a Lasting Injury to survive it. This has no effect while an ally who can act is adjacent to you, guarding you.

**GM Warning:** Being Defenseless is deadly. A 14th-level hero can be slain by a single blow from a 1st-level foe. Make sure players understand the danger, and give them a chance to stand guard over a fallen ally.

The GM decides whether a creature in a natural sleep is Unconscious.
