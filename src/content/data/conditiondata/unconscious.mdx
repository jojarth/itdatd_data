An **Unconscious** creature is senseless and helpless. When you become Unconscious, you drop whatever you are holding and become <ConditionLink id="prone" />. Prone is a separate condition: you are still Prone when you wake.

<EffectTable>
<EffectRow title="Senseless">You cannot Act, React, move, or speak, and you are unaware of your surroundings.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Limp">You automatically fail Brawn, Agility, and Precision tests, and Saving Throws vs Breath.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Exposed" href="/playersguide/08-combat/#exposed">Every foe holds Combat Supremacy against you.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Overmatched">You cannot hold Combat Supremacy against any creature.</EffectRow>
<EffectRow title="Defenseless">A foe adjacent to you deals its weapon attack damage directly to your Hit Points instead of your Grit, and you cannot choose a Lasting Injury to survive it. This has no effect while an ally who can act is adjacent to you, guarding you.</EffectRow>
</EffectTable>


The GM decides whether a creature in a natural sleep is Unconscious.
