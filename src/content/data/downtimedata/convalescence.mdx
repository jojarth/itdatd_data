datatype: downtime_event
title: Convalescence
shortDescription: Spend the week doing nothing but healing, and recover Hit Points equal to your Healing Rate + 1.
time: 1 Downtime Week
---

You devote the week to nothing but rest and healing. At the end of the week, you recover Hit Points equal to your **Healing Rate + 1**. Rests taken during that week add nothing to your Healing Track.
