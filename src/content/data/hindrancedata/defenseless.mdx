While **Defenseless**, a foe adjacent to you deals its weapon attack damage directly to your Hit Points instead of your Grit. You cannot choose to suffer a Lasting Injury to survive this damage.

This hindrance has no effect while an ally who is not Incapacitated is adjacent to you, guarding you.

**GM Warning:** Defenseless is deadly. A Defenseless 14th-level hero can be slain by a single blow from a 1st-level foe. Make sure players understand the danger before their characters become Defenseless, and give them a chance to stand guard over a fallen ally.
