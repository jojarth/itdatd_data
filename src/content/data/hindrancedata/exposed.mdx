**Exposed (Scope)** lets foes hold Combat Supremacy against you. The scope is either **all**, meaning every foe holds Combat Supremacy against you, or **adjacent**, meaning only foes adjacent to you do.

If another effect prevents creatures from holding Combat Supremacy against you, that effect overrides Exposed.
