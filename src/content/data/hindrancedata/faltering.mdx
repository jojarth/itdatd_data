While **Faltering**, every Master Action you perform costs 1 additional Action. A Master Action that normally costs 3 Actions cannot be performed at all. Master Reactions cost you 1 Action; if you have no Actions remaining, you cannot use them.

Most weapon attacks and spells are Master Actions, so a Faltering creature can still attack and cast, but more slowly.
