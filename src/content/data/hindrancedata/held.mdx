title: Held
shortDescription: You cannot move unless you drag the creature holding you.

While **Held**, you cannot move on your own. Once per round, you or the creature holding you can make an opposed test to move. The creature trying to move tests Brawn, and the other creature tests Brawn or Agility, applying the Common Combat Maneuver Modifiers. The winner can move that round and brings the other creature along; the loser cannot move that round. No test is needed if the other creature is Incapacitated.
