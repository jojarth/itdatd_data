**Hindered Movement (X)** makes every step a struggle. Each 5 feet you move costs X additional feet of movement. For example, with Hindered Movement (5), moving 5 feet costs 10 feet of your movement.

Hindered Movement from different sources adds together, but each source counts only once. Difficult terrain imposes Hindered Movement (5) while you move through it, no matter how many obstacles it contains. If you are also Prone, you suffer Hindered Movement (10).

If Hindered Movement would leave you unable to move at all, you can still use the [Struggle Forward](/playersguide/08-combat/#struggle-forward) activity to move 5 feet.
