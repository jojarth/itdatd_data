While **Shield Hampered**, you cannot actively defend with your shield. You cannot use the Shield Block reaction or benefit from the shield's Critical Protection, and its AC bonus is reduced by 1, to a minimum of +0. Its bonus to Saving Throws vs Breath still applies: even a shield you cannot raise is something to crouch behind.
