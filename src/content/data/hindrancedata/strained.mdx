datatype: hindrance
title: Strained
shortDescription: Effects you maintain cost X additional Actions to maintain.
valued: true
stacking: highest
tags:
- combat
---

**Strained (X)** makes it harder to keep your efforts going. Whenever you spend Actions to maintain or refresh an effect, such as a Battle Trance or a Spellsong, you must spend X additional Actions to do so. If you cannot pay the full cost, you cannot maintain the effect.

If another effect reduces the Actions you must spend to maintain an effect, subtract the smaller value from the larger and apply only the difference.
