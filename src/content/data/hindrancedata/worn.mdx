**Worn (Severity)** reduces your maximum Grit. The severity is either **Half**, which halves your maximum Grit, or **Quarter**, which reduces it to one quarter. Round down. Quarter is the more severe of the two.

If your current Grit is higher than your new maximum, it is reduced to match. When Worn ends, your maximum returns to normal, but your current Grit does not; you must recover it normally.
