---
datatype: injury
title: Broken Arm
shortDescription: "A bone in your arm is broken. You cannot use that arm until it heals."
maxHitPointReduction: 2
mending: 20
treatment: The bone must be set.
tags:
  - bone
---

You cannot use the broken arm. You cannot wield a weapon or shield, hold an object, or perform any task that requires two hands with it until the injury heals.
