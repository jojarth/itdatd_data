---
datatype: injury
title: Severed Tendon
shortDescription: "A severed tendon leaves one arm or leg useless."
maxHitPointReduction: 2
mending: 25
treatment: The wound must be stitched.
tags:
  - cut
---

The GM chooses an arm or a leg. You cannot use it until the injury heals. If it is a leg, you need a crutch or similar support to walk.
