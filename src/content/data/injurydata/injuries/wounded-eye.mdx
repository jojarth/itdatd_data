---
datatype: injury
title: Wounded Eye
shortDescription: "One eye is hurt and covered, robbing you of depth and sight."
maxHitPointReduction: 2
mending: 20
treatment: The wound must be dressed.
tags:
  - cut

---

You cannot see out of the injured eye. You suffer a −2 Situation penalty to ranged attack rolls and to tests that rely on sight.
