Holy water is used in rituals and as a spell component. It can be poured, loaded into an aspergillum, or thrown in a glass phial as a Splash Weapon using Sling proficiency. A thrown phial creates a 5-foot burst; affected creatures suffer the effect described by the creature or ability that reacts to holy water.
