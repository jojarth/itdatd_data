our experiences have changed how you resist danger. You may reduce your Ancestry Bonus to Saving Throws vs. Doom by up to 2 points and distribute the points you remove among any of your other saving throws.

Once you've selected this Keyword Feature, you can re-shuffle these two points by means of a downtime event. This must be a 21 consecutive day Downtime Event. It can only be done once a year.
