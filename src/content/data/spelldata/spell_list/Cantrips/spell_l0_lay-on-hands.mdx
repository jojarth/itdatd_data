import FeatureSection from '../../../../../components/FeatureSection.astro';

<FeatureSection title="Spell Effects">
    Touch a nearby creature. The target regains grit equal to 3 times your class level.

###### Creature Interactions
    If the target has the **Aeon**, **Animus**, **Cosmic**, or **Fey** keywords, the spell functions normally.

    If the target has the **Necros** or **Umbra** keywords, the spell instead deals sacred damage equal to 3 times your class level. The target is entitled to a Save vs Spell; on a success, it takes no damage.

    If the target has the **Construct** or **Elemental** keywords, the spell has no effect.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Spell Impovements">
###### When you can cast 3rd-level spells
    * You can forgo healing the target, and can instead remove a single disease that plauges the target.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Components">
    You must utter a complex prayer or plea of one to five words while laying a hand upon the target.
</FeatureSection>
