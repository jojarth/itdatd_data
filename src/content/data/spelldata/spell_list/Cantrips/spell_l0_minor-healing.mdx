datatype: spell
title: Minor Healing
level: 0
actioncost: 1 Action
castspeed: 4
range: Touch
duration: Instantaneous
classes:
    - Cleric
    - Bard
shortDescription: Your touch restores a small amount of vitality.
---
The target of this spell heals for 1 point of Grit. 

If you can cast 3rd level spells, increase the healing provided by this spell to 2 points of Grit. If you can cast 6th level spells, increase the healing provided by this spell to 3 points of Grit.
