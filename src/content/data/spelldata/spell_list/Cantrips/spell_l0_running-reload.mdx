<SpellEntry title="Running Reload">
    <FeatureSection title="Spell Effect">
        To cast this spell, you must be wielding a weapon with the Reload property. Ammunition for that weapon must be accessible and within 5 feet of you.

        This spell costs 1 Action in Phase 1 to cast. However, you may cast it as part of any Action or Reaction used to move.

        A piece of ammunition is magically drawn from its container and loaded into the weapon, leaving the weapon armed and ready to fire. 
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Components">
        <FeatureSubsection title="Casting">
        Maintain a wavering whistle while using your Casting Hand to mime drawing a piece of ammunition, setting it in place, and arming the weapon.
        </FeatureSubsection>
    </FeatureSection>
