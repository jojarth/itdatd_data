import FeatureSection from '../../../../../components/FeatureSection.astro';

<FeatureSection title="Effect">
    To cast this spell, you must be wielding a weapon with the Reload property. Ammunition for that weapon must be accessible and within 5 feet of you.

    This spell costs 1 Action to cast. However, you may cast it as part of any Action or Reaction used to move.

    The ammunition is magically drawn from its container, loaded into the weapon, and the weapon is armed and ready to fire.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Components">
    To cast this spell, you must maintain a wavering whistle while miming the motions of reloading the weapon with one hand.
</FeatureSection>
