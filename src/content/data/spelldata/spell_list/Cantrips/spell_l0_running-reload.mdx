To cast this spell successfully, you must be weilding a weapon with the Reload property, and ammo for this weapon must be accessable, and within 5 feet of you. This spell costs 1 Action to cast, however, you can cast this spell as part of any Action or Reaction used to move. 

You magically move the ammunition from it's container and into the weapon, and the weapon arms itself, becoming ready to fire.
