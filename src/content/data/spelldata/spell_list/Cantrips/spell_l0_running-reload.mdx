<SpellEntry title="Running Reload">

    <FeatureSection title="Spell Effect">
        This spell costs 1 Action in Phase 1 to cast. However, you may cast it as part of any Action or Reaction used to move. Additionally, to cast this spell, you must be wielding a weapon with the Reload property. By means of this spell, you reload your weapon, causing it to be ready to fire. If ammunition is on your person, or within 5 feet of you when you cast this spell, that ammunition is made ready as well.
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Components">

        <FeatureSubsection title="Casting">
            Maintain a wavering whistle while using your Casting Hand to mime drawing a piece of ammunition, setting it in place, and arming the weapon.
        </FeatureSubsection>

    </FeatureSection>
