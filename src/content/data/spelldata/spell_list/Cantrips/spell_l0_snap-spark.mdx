---
datatype: spell
title: Snap Spark
level: 0
actioncost: 1 Master Action
castspeed: 3
range: 30 Feet
duration: Instantaneous
classes:
  - Magic-User
shortDescription: Snapping your fingers, you attempt to cause a fire to harm a foe.
---
You snap your fingers and create a small burst of flame that appears at a target within range of the spell. The flame is short-lived, and won't catch anything aflame, but will cause pain. The target must make a Save vs Spell. If they succeed, they take no damage. If they fail, they take 1d6 + 1/2 your level (minimum of 1) points of fire damage. 

If you can cast 3rd level spells, increase the damage to 2d6 + 1/2 your level. If you can cast 6th level spells, increase teh damage to 3d6 + 1/2 your level.
