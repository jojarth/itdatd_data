<SpellEntry title="Watchful Sign">
    <FeatureSection title="Spell Effect">
        Touch a door, object, or point on a solid surface and place a sacred sign upon it. The sign is visible to you and lasts for the spell's duration. If a creature with the **Necros** or **Umbra** keyword comes within 5 feet of the sign, it flashes brightly and gives a brief chime audible to you if you are within 60 feet. The spell then ends.
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Spell Improvements">

        * Increase the spell's duration to 8 hours.
        </FeatureSubsection>
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Components">
        <FeatureSubsection title="Casting">
        Trace a small sign of your faith upon the chosen surface with your Casting Hand and whisper a prayer of vigilance.
        </FeatureSubsection>
    </FeatureSection>
</SpellEntry>
