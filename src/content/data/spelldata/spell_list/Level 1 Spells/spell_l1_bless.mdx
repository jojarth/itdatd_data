---
datatype: spell
title: Bless
level: 1
casting: 1 Master Action; Phase 4
range: 30 feet
duration: 1 minute
classes:
  - Cleric
  - Theurge
shortDescription: Improves allies attack rolls and saving throws.
---
import FeatureSection from '../../../../../components/FeatureSection.astro';

<FeatureSection title="Spell Effects">
   Select 3 targets within range of the spell that are not currently engaged in melee combat. The selected targets gain a +1 magic bonus to attack rolls and a +1 magic bonus to saving throws for the duration of the spell. 

###### Non-Player Characters
    In addition to the normal affects of this spell, casting this spell upon a non-player character will grant them a +1 magic bonus to their morale as well.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Spell Impovements">
###### When you can cast 3rd-level spells
    * You can target 4 creatures instead of 3.
###### When you can cast 5th-level spells
    * You can target 5 creatures instead of 4.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Components">
    To cast this spell, you present your holy symbol toward the target and speak a brief prayer of blessing.
</FeatureSection>
