import FeatureSection from '../../../../../components/FeatureSection.astro';

<FeatureSection title="Spell Effects">
    Choose a valid target within range that can both see and hear you, then speak a single-word command. The target must make a Save vs Spell.

    On a successful save, the spell has no effect.

    On a failed save, the target must immediately begin obeying your command. Until it has spent a total of three actions carrying out the command, one of which must be a Master Action, it may take only the actions necessary to fulfill your instruction.

###### Creature Interactions
    The target must possess both the **Animus** and **Humanoid** keywords. You must share a language with the target, and the command must be spoken in a language you both understand.

    Creatures whose level is three or more higher than yours automatically succeed on their saving throw. Creatures with a Presence attribute of 6 or lower automatically fail.

###### Issuing the Command
    The command must be a single, clear, and unambiguous word. The target follows the command to the best of its ability, but will not knowingly harm itself or directly harm one of its allies.

    If the command cannot reasonably be carried out, the target instead spends the required actions attempting to comply. For example, a creature commanded to "die" collapses to the ground and remains motionless for the duration of the effect.

    Common commands include **back**, **fall**, **flee**, **go**, **halt**, **leave**, **rest**, **run**, **sleep**, **stop**, and **surrender**.

###### Over Multiple Rounds
    If the target cannot spend all three required actions before the end of the current round, it continues obeying the command beginning in Phase 1 of the next round until all required actions have been spent.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Components">
    You must speak the command in a clear, commanding voice while pointing at the target.
</FeatureSection>
