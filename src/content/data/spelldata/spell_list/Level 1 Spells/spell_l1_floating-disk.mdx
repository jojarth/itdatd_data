<SpellEntry title="Floating Disk">

    <FeatureSection title="Spell Effect">
        Upon finishing this spell, you create a partially transparent, glowing, shield-like disk. It hovers at about waist height for a medium-sized humanoid, regardless of the size of the creature that cast it. The disk follows you wherever you go, hovering about 5 to 15 feet behind you. You have no control over it; it simply moves as you do, staying close and roughly at waist height.

        The disk can carry up to 20 EP worth of objects. If a creature attempts to mount it, the disk disappears, dropping its load unceremoniously, and reappears somewhere else within 15 feet of you. The disk can travel up normal stairs, but may have trouble with steep inclines. It has a radius of about 5 feet. If it needs to shift to fit through an opening or portal, there's a chance it will drop its cargo. For example, it can likely pass through a standard doorway with little trouble, tilting slightly if needed. If you try to squeeze through a narrow gap, however, the disk will likely turn vertical to follow you, dropping everything it carries.

        Although morbid, the disk can also transport a corpse.
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Components">

        <FeatureSubsection title="Casting">
            Turn your Casting Hand palm-up and draw a level circle in the air, then speak a phrase commanding the unseen disk to rise.
        </FeatureSubsection>

    </FeatureSection>

</SpellEntry>
