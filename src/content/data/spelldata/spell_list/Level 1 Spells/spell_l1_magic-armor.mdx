        You touch a valid creature, and that creature becomes surrounded with magic. Their AC becomes 13.
    </FeatureSection>
    <FeatureSection title="Creature Interactions">
        <FeatureSubsection title="Valid Creatures">
        * Any creature who is not currently donning armor. Additionally, this spell immediately ends if the target dons armor while it is active. 
        </FeatureSubsection>
    </FeatureSection>
    <FeatureSection title="Spell Improvements">
        <FeatureSubsection title="When you can cast 3rd-level spells">
        * Their AC becomes 14.
        </FeatureSubsection>
        <FeatureSubsection title="When you can cast 5th-level spells">
        * Their AC becomes 15.
        </FeatureSubsection>
    </FeatureSection>
