---
datatype: spell
title: Read Magic
level: 1
casting: 1 Master Action; Phase 4
range: 60 feet
duration: 1 hour
shortDescription: You are able to read and understand magical text.
classes:
  - Cleric
  - Theurge
  - Druid
  - Magic-User
  - Paladin
---
import FeatureSection from '../../../../../components/FeatureSection.astro';
import FeatureSubsection from '../../../../../components/FeatureSubsection.astro';
import SpellEntry from '../../../../../components/SpellEntry.astro';

<SpellEntry title="Read Magic">

    <FeatureSection title="Spell Effect">
        For the duration of the spell, you are able to read magical runes and text, albeit slowly. You can read about 1 page of text per minute. After reading any magical text with this spell, you can later read the same text without this spell. 

        All spell books writings are considered magical, and thus cannot be read by anyone except the author of the book, unless this spell is used to read them.
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Spell Preparation Notes">
        Any class who is required to prepare spells out of Spell Books (for example, the Magic-User), does not need access to a spell book to prepare this spell, and can prepare it from memory alone. 
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Components">

        <FeatureSubsection title="Preparation">
            Before preparing the spell, copy a line of unfamiliar script in dark ink, then study the marks until their shapes are clear. The ink is consumed when the spell is successfully prepared.
        </FeatureSubsection>

        <FeatureSubsection title="Casting">
            Trace the first line of magical writing in the air with your Casting Hand and quietly sound out its opening marks.
        </FeatureSubsection>

    </FeatureSection>

</SpellEntry>
