<SpellEntry title="Phantasmal Force">

    <FeatureSection title="Spell Effect">
        One creature within 60 feet that fails a Save vs Spell perceives an illusion for the duration. It can mislead or distract but cannot deal damage or physically restrain. The target can spend an Action to examine it and repeat its save.
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Components">

        <FeatureSubsection title="Casting">
            Describe the illusion in a low voice while shaping its outline in the air with your Casting Hand and pointing toward the target.
        </FeatureSubsection>

    </FeatureSection>
