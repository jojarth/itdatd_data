<SpellEntry title="Produce Flame">

    <FeatureSection title="Spell Effect">
        Create a flame in your hand. As an Action, direct it at one creature within 30 feet; it makes a Save vs Spell, taking 3d8 fire damage on failure or half on success. Add 1d8 at 3rd-level and another 1d8 at 5th-level spellcasting. The flame sheds light.
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Spell Improvements">

        <FeatureSubsection title="When you can cast 3rd-level spells">Increase the damage by 1d8.</FeatureSubsection>

            <FeatureSubsection title="When you can cast 5th-level spells">Increase the damage by another 1d8.</FeatureSubsection>
                </FeatureSection>

                <FeatureSection title="Components">

                    <FeatureSubsection title="Casting">
                        Turn your Casting Hand palm-up and speak a word of ignition as a flame kindles above it. To hurl it, flick your hand toward the target.
                    </FeatureSubsection>

                </FeatureSection>

                </SpellEntry>
