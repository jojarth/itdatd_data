<SpellEntry title="Web">

    <FeatureSection title="Spell Effect">
        Fill a 20-foot-square area within 60 feet with sticky webs. The area is difficult terrain for the spell's duration.

        Each creature inside the area when the spell takes effect makes a Save vs Spell. On a failure, it becomes <ConditionLink id="restrained" />. On a success, it becomes <ConditionLink id="entangled" />.

        **Caught Again.** The first time each round that a creature moves within the web, it must make a Save vs Binding. On a failure, it becomes caught more tightly: a free creature becomes Entangled, and an Entangled creature becomes Restrained.

        **Breaking Free.** A Restrained creature can spend 3 Actions, starting in Phase 1, to make a Save vs Binding. If it succeeds, it tears partly loose: it is no longer Restrained, but it becomes Entangled. An Entangled creature can spend 1 Action to make a Brawn or Agility test. If it succeeds, the Entangled condition ends. Both conditions end when the spell does.

        Webs are flammable.
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Components">

        <FeatureSubsection title="Casting">
            Spread the fingers of your Casting Hand and cast it outward like a net while speaking a word of binding.
        </FeatureSubsection>

    </FeatureSection>

</SpellEntry>
