---
datatype: spell
title: Counterspell
level: 3
casting: 1 Master Reaction
range: 60 feet
duration: Instant
shortDescription: You tear apart a spell as it is being cast.
classes:
  - Magic-User
---
import FeatureSection from '../../../../../components/FeatureSection.astro';
import FeatureSubsection from '../../../../../components/FeatureSubsection.astro';
import SpellEntry from '../../../../../components/SpellEntry.astro';

<SpellEntry title="Counterspell">

    <FeatureSection title="Spell Effect">
        You can cast this spell when you see or hear a creature within range casting a spell. You tear the threads of that spell before they can be tied.

        The caster must make a Saving Throw vs Spell. They gain a +1 Situation bonus for each level their spell is above 3rd. On a failure, their spell is countered: it takes no effect, and their Actions, Master Action, and spell slot are spent. On a success, their spell is cast normally.
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Components">
        <FeatureSubsection title="Preparation">
            Study the spell while tying a cord into a knot and pulling it free, again and again, until it unties at a touch.
        </FeatureSubsection>

        <FeatureSubsection title="Casting">
            Snap your Casting Hand closed toward the caster, as if catching something thrown, and speak a single sharp word.
        </FeatureSubsection>
    </FeatureSection>

</SpellEntry>
