import FeatureSection from '../../../../../components/FeatureSection.astro';

<FeatureSection title="Effects">
    To cast this spell, spend 1 Master Action beginning on Phase 4. You conjure 6 magical bolts of force that hover above your head for 1 hour. These bolts cannot be targeted or otherwise interacted with. They may only be launched as described below.

###### Launching Bolts
    By spending 1 Action beginning on Phase 2, you may launch any number of your remaining bolts at creatures you can see within the spell's range. If you launch multiple bolts with the same Action, you may divide them among different targets in range, but you must declare all targets before rolling any damage. The bolts strike unerringly and deals 1d4 + 2 force damage. Once a bolt is launched, it is destroyed. 
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Spell Impovements">
###### When you can cast 6th-level spells
    * You create 8 bolts instead of 6.
    * Each bolt deals 1d6 + 2 points of force damage.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Components">
    To cast this spell, you must chant the spell aloud while twirling an upraised finger in a circle above your head.

    Whenever you launch a bolt, you must again chant the spell while pointing at the target. In both cases, the verbal component is accompanied by a sweeping gesture that begins at your side and ends with your finger directed toward its destination.
</FeatureSection>
