---
datatype: "weapon_feature"
title: "Advantageous"
shortDescription: "Increases damage against a foe suffering from a negative Condition."
---

When you deal damage to a creature suffering from a negative Condition, increase this weapon's damage dice by 1 step. Combat Supremacy does not satisfy this Feature because it is not a Condition. A negative Condition satisfies this Feature normally, even if that Condition also causes one or more creatures to hold Combat Supremacy against the affected creature.
