---
datatype: "weapon_feature"
title: "Bold"
shortDescription: "Risks a confirmed threat to turn an ordinary critical hit into triple damage."
---

When you critically hit a creature that is not benefiting from Critical Protection, you can treat the result as a Critical Threat instead. If you confirm the threat, roll this weapon's damage dice three times instead of twice. If you fail to confirm it, the attack becomes a normal hit.
