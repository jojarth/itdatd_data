---
datatype: "weapon_feature"
title: "Breaching"
shortDescription: "Overpowers wooden shields and serves as an effective chopping tool."
---

A wooden shield does not grant Critical Protection against attacks made with this weapon. Other sources of Critical Protection function normally. When a wooden shield is used to Shield Block your attack, increase its Break Chance by 1. You can also use this weapon to chop wood without suffering an improvised-tool penalty.
