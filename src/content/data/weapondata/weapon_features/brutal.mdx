---
datatype: "weapon_feature"
title: "Brutal"
shortDescription: "Raises the minimum result of the weapon's damage dice."
---

When you deal damage with this weapon, the minimum result you can roll on a damage dice is increased. If you roll lower than this minimum result, treat the damage dice as if it were the result.

If your damage dice is a d4, the minimum is 2. If your damage dice is d6 or d8, the minimum is 3. If your damage dice is d10 or d12, the minimum is 4.
