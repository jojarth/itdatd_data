---
datatype: "weapon_feature"
title: "Cleave"
shortDescription: "Allows follow-up attacks after killing a creature."
---

When you successfully kill a creature, you can immediately make another attack with the same weapon against a creature in range, but you decrease the damage dice by 1 step.You can cleave a maximum number of times equal to the number of damage dice you roll, but decrease the damage dice by 1 dice step for each cleave you perform.
