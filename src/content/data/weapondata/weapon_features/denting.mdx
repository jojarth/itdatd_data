---
datatype: "weapon_feature"
title: "Denting"
shortDescription: "Batters plate armor and compromises metal shields."
---

A metal shield does not grant Critical Protection against attacks made with this weapon. Other sources of Critical Protection function normally. When a metal shield is used to Shield Block your attack, increase its Break Chance by 1. When you damage a creature wearing plate armor, increase this weapon's damage dice by 1 step.
