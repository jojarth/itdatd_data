---
datatype: "weapon_feature"
title: "Grappling"
shortDescription: "Allows Grapple maneuvers to be performed with the weapon."
---

You can attempt a Grapple Combat Maneuver against a creature within this weapon's reach without requiring a free hand. Use Brawn for your Opposed Attribute Test, or Precision if this weapon possesses the Finesse Property. While the weapon maintains the Grapple, it is committed and cannot attack or Grapple another creature.
