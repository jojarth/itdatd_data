---
datatype: "weapon_feature"
title: "Leverage"
shortDescription: "Rewards Engaging or Reach attacks with increased damage and closer-range control."
---

This feature must be paired with a weapon that has the Engaging weapon feature, or Reach weapon property. When you attack a creature after utilizing the movement provided by the Engaging Feature, increase the damage dice by 1 dice step. Additionally, you do not suffer penalties for attacking a creature within 5 feet of you with a weapon that has the Reach Property.
