---
datatype: "weapon_feature"
title: "Leverage"
shortDescription: "Rewards committed entry and mitigates a Reach weapon's close-range weakness."
---

This Feature must be paired with Engaging or Reach. When you attack after using the movement provided by Engaging, increase this weapon's damage dice by 1 step. You also ignore the damage-die reduction for attacking within 5 feet with a Reach weapon, although the weapon still does not threaten creatures within 5 feet.
