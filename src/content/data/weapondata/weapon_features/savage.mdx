---
datatype: "weapon_feature"
title: "Savage"
shortDescription: "Allows rerolling and potentially increasing selected damage dice."
---

After rolling for damage, select 1 of the damage dice you rolled. You can reroll that damage dice. When your combat bonus is high enough to roll additional damage dice, the selected dice can also be increased by 1 dice step. If you critically hit, you can select a second dice to reroll.
