## Description

A cosh is a short, rigid weighted truncheon, often made with a metal core beneath leather or cord wrapping. It hits harder than a sap but is less suited to concealment and delicate knock-out work.

## Cross-Training

Characters at least Proficient with **Clubs and Maces** or the **Sap** are Cross-Trained with the Cosh. When you are at least Proficient with the Cosh, you are Cross-Trained with **Clubs and Maces** and the **Sap**.
