## Description

The goedendag is a stout staff-club reinforced with iron and capped by a thrusting spike. It combines the stopping force of a heavy club with a point capable of meeting cavalry or exploiting an opening.

## Cross-Training

Characters at least Proficient with **Clubs and Maces** or **Spears** are Cross-Trained with the Goedendag. When you are at least Proficient with the Goedendag, you are Cross-Trained with **Clubs and Maces** and **Spears**.
