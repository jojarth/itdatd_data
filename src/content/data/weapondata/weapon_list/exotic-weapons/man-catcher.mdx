## Description

A man-catcher or catchpole uses a forked, hinged, or sprung head to seize a person at the end of a shaft. Such devices are associated with guards and captors who value restraint over killing, and their mechanisms vary widely.

## Cross-Training

Characters at least Proficient with **Polearms** are Cross-Trained with the Man-Catcher. When you are at least Proficient with the Man-Catcher, you are Cross-Trained with **Polearms**.
