## Description

A punch dagger has a transverse grip that places its short blade in front of the wielder's fist. It is driven with compact punching motions rather than conventional dagger thrusts, making it effective in cramped fighting but sufficiently unusual to require its own proficiency.

## Cross-Training

Characters at least Proficient with **Brawling** or **Daggers** are Cross-Trained with the Punch Dagger. When you are at least Proficient with the Punch Dagger, you are Cross-Trained with **Brawling** and **Daggers**.
