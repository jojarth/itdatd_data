## Description

A sap is a compact, flexible striking weapon: a small leather pouch or layered pad weighted with shot. It is easy to conceal and meant to stun rather than maim, but its specialized use places it among Exotic Weapons rather than standard military arms.

## Cross-Training

Characters at least Proficient with **Clubs and Maces** or the **Cosh** are Cross-Trained with the Sap. When you are at least Proficient with the Sap, you are Cross-Trained with **Clubs and Maces** and the **Cosh**.
