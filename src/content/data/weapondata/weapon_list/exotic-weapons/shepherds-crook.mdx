## Description

A shepherd's crook is a long staff ending in a pronounced hook used to manage animals. In practiced hands the shaft strikes like a staff while the crook catches limbs, clothing, and equipment.

## Cross-Training

Characters at least Proficient with **Staves** are Cross-Trained with the Shepherd's Crook. When you are at least Proficient with the Shepherd's Crook, you are Cross-Trained with **Staves**.
