---
datatype: "weapon_property"
title: "Lashing"
shortDescription: "Changes weapon damage according to the target's armor."
---

Lashing weapons damage dice changes when dealing damage to creatures wearing certain armors: increase the damage dice by 2 steps if your target is unarmored, decrease the damage dice by 1 step if the target is wearing medium armor, decreased the damage dice by 2 steps if the creature is wearing heavy armor. Creatures wearing plate armor are immune to lashing weapon damage.
