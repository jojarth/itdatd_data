---
datatype: "weapon_property"
title: "Light"
shortDescription: "Allows a compact weapon to serve as a secondary weapon in two-weapon fighting."
---

This weapon can serve as the secondary weapon when engaging in two-weapon fighting. A Small creature must use two Light weapons and must designate a Light weapon with a bundled EP value as its secondary weapon. See Two-Weapon Fighting in Chapter 6: Equipment for the complete rules.
