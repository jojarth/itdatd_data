---
datatype: "weapon_property"
title: "Reach"
shortDescription: "Extends melee reach to 10 feet with penalties at close range."
---

When wielding a weapon with the Reach property, your melee range with that weapon is 10 feet. However: you reduce your damage dice by 1 dice step if you attack a creature within 5 feet of you, and do not threaten squares 5 feet out from you either.
