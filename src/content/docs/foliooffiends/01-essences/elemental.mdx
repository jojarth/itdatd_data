Out in the hill country, folk leave a cup of milk on the hearth for the fire, and a coin at the river ford for the water. Ask them why and they'll shrug and say it's what their parents did. Most of the time, the milk and the coin go to nobody. Once in a while, the fire drinks.

Elemental creatures are beings whose bodies *are* a fundamental force or substance. A fire Elemental doesn't live in the flame or wield it; it is the flame, given will and motion. Some are made entirely of their element, and others hold a stable shape around an elemental core. Mortals often mistake them for spirits, minor gods, or the landscape itself coming to life. Sometimes they're worshipped, sometimes bargained with, and more often feared as disasters that walk.

## Telling Them Apart

### Elemental or Animus?
A fire-breathing salamander is Animus. It hatched from an egg, it eats, and the fire comes from inside its body. A creature that is literally sustained by living flame, and would die if the flame went out, is Elemental.

### Elemental or Fey?
The river-spirit that demands a coin at the ford and drowns those who don't pay is Fey. It's bound by custom, and its power comes from the bargain. A wave that rises from the river with a will of its own, and no rules at all, is Elemental. Fey creatures care about promises. Elementals usually don't understand them.

### Elemental or Construct?
If someone built it, it's a Construct, even if it's made of stone. If it rose on its own, it's Elemental.

## Example Creatures
- **A living bonfire** that wanders the moors on midsummer night, looking for more to burn.
- **A storm with a will**, rolling down from the peaks and following one particular traveler for days.
- **Stone-walkers**, slow creatures of rock and ore that move through the deep veins and sometimes surface in dwarven mines.
- **A smoke-thing** that rose from a burning city and never quite settled.
- **Frost-wraiths**, Elementals of ice and wind that haunt high passes in winter. Despite the name, they are not undead.

## Running Elemental Creatures
Elementals are forces first and creatures second. Play them like weather. A fire Elemental spreads and consumes; a water Elemental floods, erodes, and drags things down. They rarely plot, but they're always dangerous, and the terrain around them should change as the fight goes on.

Many Elementals are drawn to or tied to a place, a season, or an event. That gives the party ways to deal with them that don't involve hit points: wait out the storm, dam the stream, or smother the fire. Reward players who think about what the element is, not just how much damage it deals.
