In every village in Cerrix, the dead are buried properly. The rites are said, the coins are placed, the graves are dug deep, and nobody walks through the cemetery after dark if they can help it. Everyone knows what happens when the rites go wrong. Most of them have heard the stories. Some of them have seen it.

Necros creatures are kept going by death itself. They are ghosts, revenants, vampires, and the walking dead: things that should have stopped, but didn't. Some remember who they were and still want what they wanted in life. Others are little more than hunger, obedience, or one unfinished purpose dragging a body or a spirit forward.

Necros is an Essence, not a judgment. The ghost of a murdered child, an honored ancestor guarding the family tomb, and a knight who refuses to rest until his oath is fulfilled are all Necros, and none of them is evil.

## Necros Gameplay
All Necros creatures share the following traits.

## Telling Them Apart

### Necros or Construct?
See [Construct](/foliooffiends/01-essences/construct/). In short: if death itself is keeping it up, it's Necros. If someone used corpses as building material, it's a Construct.

### Necros or Umbra?
A Necros creature was usually something else first, and death changed it. An Umbra creature was born from, or shaped by, corruption and malice. A vampire is Necros, no matter how cruel it is. The demon that whispered the vampire's first victim into the grave is Umbra.

### Necros or Fey?
See [Fey](/foliooffiends/01-essences/fey/). If an oath or a place keeps it going, it may be Fey. If death keeps it going, it's Necros.

## Example Creatures
- **The walking dead**, from shambling corpses to skeletons that still remember how to hold a spear.
- **Barrow-wights**, the restless dead of ancient chieftains, guarding grave goods no living hand should touch.
- **Ghosts** tied to the place they died, replaying their last moments or seeking justice.
- **Vampires**, the most feared of the dead, who hunger for the warmth of the living.
- **Revenants**, who rise for one purpose, usually vengeance, and rest only when it's done.
- **The ancestral guardian**, an honored ghost that watches over a family's tomb and only troubles those who disturb it.

## Running Necros Creatures
Necros creatures are cold, and that matters. Characters with Infravision see many of them as empty voids against the background, which can be more frightening than seeing them clearly. Describe the chill, the smell of old earth, and the silence where breathing should be.

Ask what each Necros creature wants. Mindless dead want to feed or obey. Ghosts usually want something that was left unfinished, and a party that discovers it may be able to lay the ghost to rest without a fight. Vampires and other intelligent dead want much the same things they wanted in life, only more, and forever.

:::tip[Burial matters]{icon="information"}
In ITD&TD, how the dead are treated matters. Battlefields left unburied, graves robbed, and rites skipped are all good reasons for the dead to rise. When the party loots a tomb, let them wonder whether they've just started a story they won't like.
:::
