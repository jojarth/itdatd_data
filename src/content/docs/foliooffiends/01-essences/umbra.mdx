The old women in the market will tell you that demons are everywhere. The fever, the failed harvest, the son who ran off with the tinker's daughter: all demons. They're mostly wrong. Mostly.

Umbra creatures are made of corruption and malice. They're born from, sustained by, or shaped by the forces that work against the proper order of existence. Demons, devils, possessing spirits, and things that grew out of cruelty, temptation, or spiritual rot are all Umbra. Their very presence tends to harm, corrupt, deceive, or degrade the world around them.

What makes Umbra creatures truly dangerous is that they are often easy to understand. Unlike Cosmic creatures, their motives are rarely alien. They want power, souls, worship, suffering, or freedom, and they know exactly what mortals want in return.

## Umbra Gameplay
All Umbra creatures share the following traits.

## Telling Them Apart

### Umbra or Cosmic?
See [Cosmic](/foliooffiends/01-essences/cosmic/). In short: an Umbra creature works against the world's order. A Cosmic creature doesn't fit it at all. A demon wants your soul. A Cosmic creature may not know you have one.

### Umbra or Necros?
See [Necros](/foliooffiends/01-essences/necros/). In short: death changed a Necros creature into what it is. An Umbra creature was corruption from the start, or was remade by it.

### Umbra or a Wicked Mortal?
A cultist who sold his soul, a sorcerer who sacrifices children, and a tyrant who rules through terror are all Animus. Some may be **Defiled**, if dark powers have personally marked them. None of them is Umbra. A mortal only becomes Umbra if corruption replaces the thing that keeps them alive, and that should be very rare.

### Possession
When an Umbra creature possesses a mortal, there are two creatures in one body: the Animus host and the Umbra spirit. Decide which one a spell or effect touches based on what it does. A spell that affects the body affects the host. A spell that drives out spirits affects the Umbra creature.

## Example Creatures
- **Demons** of every kind, from small, spiteful imps to towering lords of ruin.
- **Devils**, who prefer contracts to claws and are always the better negotiator.
- **Possessing spirits** that slip into the weak-willed and wear them like clothing.
- **A shade born from a massacre**, made of the malice of everyone who died there, and of everyone who killed them.
- **The tempter**, who appears to the desperate as a kind stranger, offering exactly what they want for a price they won't understand until it's too late.

## Running Umbra Creatures
Umbra creatures should feel like a threat to more than the characters' Grit and Hit Points. They tempt, bargain, deceive, and corrupt. A fight with a demon is dangerous. A conversation with one can be worse.

Don't let every Umbra creature be a brute. The most memorable ones are patient. They offer the fighter strength, the thief a way out, and the priest a miracle for a dying friend. Give players real choices, and let them live with the consequences.

:::tip[Keep demons rare]{icon="information"}
In a swords & sorcery world, most evil is mortal evil. Umbra creatures should be the exception: the thing behind the cult, the voice in the sorcerer's mirror, the horror that waits at the bottom of the deepest dungeon. If demons are around every corner, they stop being frightening.
:::
