Welcome to In the Deep and the Dark. An OSR-Adjacent game designed by Jourdan Leach (akraid@gmail.com). By design, this game eschews the more modern rules-lite system, and instead looks to be rules-moderate, proceedure-heavy, and all with the sustained capacity to tell captivating stories with you and your friends..

Included with ITD&TD is the world of Cerrix, a harsh world that lies somewhere between swords and sorcery and high fantasy. Adventures in Cerrix are brutal and dangerous, but if you can survive you will have a story worth telling!

Please select a book to begin!
