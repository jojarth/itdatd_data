The 4 example characters (Astril, Brynjolf, Cerrig, and Derrian) have been included below to see character creation being done for a variety of classes. Use the tabs below to navigate to each character's class.

Our example players are Sarah, Maria, James, and Ronnie. Our example GM is Clint.

## Character List

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Astril the Half-Elven Cleric">
    Sarah is starting to play ITD&TD with her partner, Maria, an experienced player, but is otherwise a complete beginer. 

    ### Step 1. Determine Attributes
    Sarah rolls 4d6 and gets a 3, 6, 3, and 1. She must select a dice to discard, and she discards the 1. She then adds the remaining 3, 6 and 3 to get 12. This is her character's brawn score. She repeats the process 7 times to get her abiliy scores in order: brawn, heartiness, agility, precision, logic, reason, savvy, and presence. 

    <div class="orange-table">
    | **Score**      | **Roll 1** | **Roll 2** | **Roll 3** | **Roll 4** | **Discard** | **Total** |
    |:--------------:|:----------:|:----------:|:----------:|:----------:|:-----------:|:---------:|
    | **Brawn**      | 3          | 6          | 3          | 1          | Roll 4 (1)  | 12        |
    | **Heartiness** | 1          | 5          | 3          | 2          | Roll 1 (1)  | 10        |
    | **Agility**    | 4          | 4          | 3          | 4          | Roll 3 (3)  | 12        |
    | **Precision**  | 3          | 1          | 5          | 3          | Roll 2 (1)  | 11        |
    | **Logic**      | 4          | 6          | 4          | 4          | Roll 1 (4)  | 14        |
    | **Reason**     | 3          | 6          | 2          | 5          | Roll 3 (2)  | 14        |
    | **Savvy**      | 1          | 3          | 4          | 3          | Roll 1 (1)  | 10        |
    | **Presence**   | 1          | 3          | 1          | 4          | Roll 1(1)   | 8         |
    </div>

    Sarah is very happy with these scores, with no poor scores (a roll of 8 still constitutes no penalties). This set of rolls would make all classes be great. However, Sarah doesn't need to select her class quite yet, she has a little time to think. 

    She records her scores on her Character sheet.  
    * Her Brawn Score of 12 gives her no bonuses or penalties to attack rolls, damage rolls, or encumbrance. It is not high enough to modifier her X-in-Y tests when using force.
    * Her heartiness score of 10 gives her 4 hit points, and contributes 3 points to her Grit. 
    * Her agility score of 12 gives her no bonuses or penalties to movement speed or armor class.
    * Her precision score of 11 gives her no bonuses or penalties to attack rolls, damage rolls, or encumbrance. It is not high enough to modifier her X-in-Y tests when using manipulation.
    * Her logic score of 14 means she can speak her naitive languages just fine, but does not start play literate unless her class allows her to.
    * Her reason score of 14 means she has no bonuses or penalties to spells that alter the mind. It is not high enough to modifier her X-in-Y tests when using her acuity.
    * Her Savvy score of 10 means she has no impact on NPC reactions, she increases the maximum number of henchmen she can hire by +2, and any henchmen she hires has a loyalty base of 5. 
    * Her presence score of 8 gives her an additional +3 contribution to her grit, increases the maximum number of henchmen she can hire by +2, and increases her henchment's loyalty base by +1.

    ### Step 2. Determine ABCs
    Looking at all the ancestries, Sarah is torn between playing an elf and a human. She decides to simply be a half-elf. Her ancestry is decided, and she writes down several peices of information on her character sheet:

    * She has the _animus, fey, humanoid, elf,_ and _human_ ancestry tags.
    * Her base move speed is 25 feet. Her agility does not change this.
    * She notes she is of medium size.
    * She notes her naitive languages are common and elvish. She gains no additional languages based on her logic.
    * She notes that she has low-light vision.
    * She notes that she has the Dual Heritage, and Silver Heart ancestry features.
    * Upon selecting her class, she will gain access to a class keyword feature, but she has not selected a class yet.

    For a background, she decides her half-elf was a farmer. Her GM should take that into account when having her preform attribute tests. 

    Finally, she can select a class. She had no poor rolls for her attributes, but being either a magic-user or cleric would give her a bonus to experience points earned, due to her high logic and reason scores. She settles on Cleric. She does the following

    * First, she handles her class gameplay
      * She adds the _priest_ class keyword to her list of keywords.
      * She notes her reason attribute is high enough to grant her a +5% bonus to XP gains.
    * Next, she deals with her proficiencies.
      * She is proficient with light, medium, and heavy armor, noting it on her sheet.
      * She is also proficient with bucklers, small, and medium shields, noting it on her sheet.
      * She starts play Familiar with Brawling, Clubs and Maces, Hammers and Picks, Two-Handed Bludgeons, Flails, Staves, and Crossbows. Because her Combat Bonus (listed below) is +2, she gains 4 Weapon Proficiency Points to spend on purchasing weapon proficiencies, and she can have a maximum Proficiency Level of Proficient.
        * She spends 1 point to upgrade Clubs and Maces from Familiar to Proficient.
        * She spends 1 point to upgrade her flails from familiar to proficient. 
        * She spends 1 point to upgrade her bows from non-proficient to familiar. 
        * She spends 1 point to upgrade her bows from familiar to proficient. 
    * Looking at the Cleric Class table, she notes:
      * She gains 1d6 + 2 grit. She rolls a 2 (4 total from her class). 
      * She has a Combat Bonus of +2. 
      * She has a Skill Bonus of +0. 
      * Her Save vs Doom is 12.
      * Her Save vs Device is 14.
      * Her Save vs Binding is 12.
      * Her Save vs Breath is 14.
      * Her Save vs Spell is 12. 
    * She also gains the Holy Symbol and Turn Undead feature.

  Now that she has selected a class, she goes back to her half-elf ancestry to button up a few items. First off, she gains an additional benefit based on the keyword she gained by her class (_priest_). As a half-elf, she can select if she wants the human or elven feature. She selected the human feature, allowing her to upgrade her armor or shield proficeny by 1 step heavier. She selectes armor, allowing her to become proficient with plate armor. 

  Next, she has to roll for her age. A Half-Elf priest has a base age of 18, and rolls 3d8 to add to that. She rolls a 3, 6, and 2 (11). 18 + 11 = 29. Her cleric is 29 years old, putting her in her Prime, and allowing for some attribute adjustments. 

  * When she was young, she was allowed to increase 1 attribute by +1 in exchange for a -1 to a different attribute, if she wished to. She selects reason for a +1, and precision for -1. She now has a 15 Reason and 10 Precision. 
  * When she reached her Prime, she was able to select 2 attributes for a +1 each. She selected reason and heartiness. This brings her Reason up to 16, and her heartiness up to 11. 
    * With her Reason score reaching 16, she gains 2 benefits. First off, she gains a +1-in-Y to certain tests, when her acuity is called into question. Second, as a cleric, she gains +10% bonus to all XP earned (up from 5%).
    * With her Heartiness reaching 11, she increases her maximum hit points to 5. Her grit contribution remains the same.
