---
title: Backgrounds
---
Backgrounds are what your character did before they became an adventurer, or what they do while not adventuring. A background is a profession, trade, role, or field of training that describes what your character knows how to do.

When Attribute Tests come up, your background may allow you to apply part or all of your Skill Bonus to the test. Chapter 7 contains additional information on Attribute Tests.

Effectively, your selected background should only influence what kinds of things your character is or was good at. Backgrounds are not set in stone, but rather a short description of how you lived before you started to adventure. If a background you want is not in the example list below, make sure the GM knows what it is.

Few backgrounds should be tied to your ancestry, although they do exist. For example, elves would _never_ allow a member of another ancestry to be a tree keeper.

Often, a list of backgrounds can be found in the campaign guide if you are playing in one. However, you should be able to take any medieval type of profession and call it a background. You should work with your GM in understanding what kinds of backgrounds are allowed.

A background should never allow a character to participate in all skill tests. The goal is for characters to all have something to contribute, but never overtake other characters in specialized fields.

For example, if someone attempted to select "Royalty" as a background, they should not immediately gain all the benefits a royal person would actually have. They are an adventurer now, after all. However, they should be allowed to apply their Skill Bonus to Attribute Tests when things a royal might have learned come into play. Diplomacy, history, horseback riding, and courtly manners all come to mind.
