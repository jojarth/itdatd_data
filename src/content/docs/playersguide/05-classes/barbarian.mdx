Barbarians come from beyond the walls and roads of the settled lands. They are hunters, raiders, and wanderers who trust their instincts over books and banners, and who survive where softer folk would perish.

## Barbarian Gameplay
Below is a list of information needed to play a barbarian at the various levels of play.

    A barbarian relies on raw might and the endurance to keep going long after others have tired. Your prime requisites are **Brawn** and **Heartiness**, and you gain bonus experience points based on these scores.

    - If all of your Prime Requisites are 13 or higher, you gain a +5% bonus to all experience points earned.
    - If all of your Prime Requisites are 16 or higher, you gain a +10% bonus to all experience points earned.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Sudden Strike"
  subeyebrow="2nd Level Barbarian Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
When you strike a creature that you hold Combat Supremacy against, you deal an additional 1d4 Ambush damage. If you do so when making a ranged or thrown attack, the target must also be within 30 feet or the weapon's first range increment, whichever is shorter.

  <FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 7th Level">
        * Increase the additional Ambush damage to **2d4**.
      </FeatureSubsection>
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 14th Level">
        * Increase the additional Ambush damage to **3d4**.
      </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Hide in Naught but Flora"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Barbarian Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, Barbarians have learned to become nearly undiscoverable while in nature. While other allies can be stealthy and hide behind objects, all a Barbarian needs to be able to hide is a thicket or full-cover foliage. Barbarians must be wearing light or no armor that is of similar color to the foliage they wish to hide in to perform this action, and must remain stationary once hidden. 

This action does not allow a Barbarian to disappear into flora, and follows the normal rules of stealth, but allows Barbarians to hide in situations that others would not be able to. 
</GameplayCard>

    :::tip[Barbarian Skills]{icon="information"}
    A Barbarian using the Hide in Naught but Flora, Climb Sheer Surfaces, and Move in Utter Silence features does not automatically succeed at these tests. A GM should still make the call for attribute tests.

    However, these features let barbarians perform functions above and beyond what other classes can. For example, a fighter simply cannot vanish into a thicket; they must find an object to hide behind. A fighter can move with stealth, but not so silently they make no noise. A fighter can climb a sheer surface, but only if they have a rope or other means of climbing available to them.

    Effectively, these barbarian skills allow a barbarian to perform beyond other classes in wilderness stealth and movement, but they do not negate the need to test the barbarian.
    :::

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Climb Sheer Surfaces"
  subeyebrow="5th Level Barbarian Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 5th level, a Barbarian can climb upon surfaces that seemingly have no handholds. While other allies may be able to scale walls with good handholds or ropes, Barbarians need no such things to be able to scale a wall. Barbarians must be wearing light or no armor, and be lightly encumbered or less to perform this action. 

Barbarians who meet these conditions can climb any walls. They must follow all other rules for climbing, but are able to scale surfaces thought to be impossible to scale unless by magic.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Move in Utter Silence"
  subeyebrow="7th Level Barbarian Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 7th level, while attempting to be stealthy, a Barbarian can move so utterly silently that they make no noise. While other allies can be stealthy and quiet, Barbarians make absolutely no perceptible noises. Barbarians must be lightly encumbered or less to perform this action, and must be wearing light or no armor.

Barbarians who meet these conditions can stealth around while making absolutely no noise, and cannot be discovered by means of noise detection.
</GameplayCard>
