Berserkir fight in a fury that others fear to stand beside. Whether driven by a warrior cult, an oath, or something darker in the blood, they hurl themselves into battle and give up caution for raw, overwhelming violence.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Berserker Fury"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Berserkir Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
As an activity costing 2 Actions, starting in Phase 2, you can enter a Berserker Fury. You gain the Battle Trance condition for as long as your Berserker Fury lasts. You cannot enter your Berserker Fury while under the Slow or Sluggish conditions, although gaining those conditions while in your Fury will not cause it to end. 


<FeatureSection title="Battle Trance Effects">
  * Each time you take Grit damage, reduce the damage you take by 3. Each time you take Hit Point damage, reduce the damage you take by 1.
  * You cannot use the Shield Block reaction while in this Battle Trance. You still gain all other benefits of your shield.
  * You cannot cast spells, read spell scrolls, use magical rods, staves, or wands, or perform delicate tasks or tasks that require concentration. 
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Maintaining the Battle Trance">
  * Each round after the round you enter it, you must spend 1 Action to maintain your Berserker Fury.
  * If you have not maintained your Berserker Fury by the end of Phase 9, it ends.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Ending the Battle Trance">
  * Your Berserker Fury lasts a number of rounds equal to your Heartiness score. You can end it early at any time (no Action required).
  * When your Berserker Fury ends for any reason, you are Slow (2) until the end of the following round.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 5th Level">
    * Each time you take Grit damage, reduce the damage you take by 5.
  </FeatureSubsection>
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 9th Level">
    * Each time you take Grit damage, reduce the damage you take by 7.
    * If you use a Master Action to make a melee weapon attack, or take any damage, during a round, your Berserker Fury is maintained for that round without spending an Action.
  </FeatureSubsection>
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 13th Level">
    * When your Berserker Fury ends, you are Slow (1) instead of Slow (2).
  </FeatureSubsection>
</FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

Upon reaching 5th level, while you are under any Battle Trance, all melee weapons you wield are treated as if they had the Cleave feature. You must be at least Familiar with the weapon to do so.

If the weapon already has the Cleave feature, the additional attack's damage dice are not reduced.
