## Cleric Features
As a cleric, you gain the following class features.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Holy Symbol"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Cleric Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Many of your class features require you to present your holy symbol. A holy symbol is a physical representation of your faith and devotion. It may take nearly any form, such as an amulet, prayer beads, a reliquary, or another relic sacred to your beliefs.

To present your holy symbol, you must hold it aloft in clear view. This requires one hand. Alternatively, you may emblazon your holy symbol upon a shield. Wielding and openly displaying such a shield allows you to present your holy symbol.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Turn Undead"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Cleric Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Clerics can channel sacred power to drive back the undead. To use this ability, you must openly present a symbol of your faith. **Turn Undead** requires **1 Master Action** and may be performed beginning in **Phase 1**. You must present your holy symbol to Turn Undead. 

  <FeatureSection title="Determine Highest Level">
    When you attempt to Turn Undead, first determine the highest-level Valid Creature you can affect.

    Roll **1d20** and compare the result to your **Presence** Attribute using the table below. The result determines the highest creature level that can be affected by this attempt.

    Valid Creatures **4 or more levels higher** than your Cleric Level cannot be affected. Likewise, Valid Creatures **3 or more levels lower** than your Cleric Level are always affected, regardless of your roll.

    <div class="yellow-table compact-table">
    |        **d20 Result**        | **Highest Creature Level** |
    | :-------------------------- | :------------------------: |
    | **Presence +6** | Cleric Level -2 |
    | **Presence +3** | Cleric Level -1 |
    | **Presence**    | Cleric Level    |
    | **Presence -3** | Cleric Level +1 |
    | **Presence -6** | Cleric Level +2 |
    | **Presence -9** | Cleric Level +3 |
    </div>
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Determine Turning Capacity">
    Next, determine the total number of creature levels you can affect. Roll **2d6** and add **half your Cleric Level** (rounded down). The result is your **Turning Capacity**.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Turn Undead Effect">
    Beginning with the lowest-level Valid Creature within **60 feet** that can see you, subtract each affected creature's level from your Turning Capacity until you either have insufficient capacity to affect another creature or there are no more Valid Creatures.

    Affected creatures immediately flee from you by the safest available route. Instead of fleeing, creatures that are **5 or more levels lower** than your Cleric Level are instantly destroyed, crumbling to dust.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Creature Interactions"> 
    <FeatureSubsection title="Valid Creatures">
      * A creature with the **Necros** keyword is a Valid Creature.
    </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="spellcasting"
  title="Spellcasting"
  subeyebrow="2nd Level Cleric Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Through prayer and unwavering faith, you have learned to prepare sacred miracles within your mind. Each miracle is a blessing that can be held only temporarily. When invoked, it is expended and slips from memory until granted again through prayer.

Your spells are **clerical spells**, and you use the **Sacred** casting method. You otherwise follow the general rules in [Chapter 09: Spellcasting](/playersguide/09-spellcasting/).

  <FeatureSection title="Spell Preparation">
  As part of a Safe Rest, you may perform a personal ritual to prepare your spells. During this ritual, you offer prayers to a higher power and beseech it to grant you sacred miracles.

  You may prepare any spell on the Cleric spell list for which you have a spell slot of the appropriate level. The spells you prepare take effect when you complete the Safe Rest. If the Safe Rest is not completed, the spells are not prepared.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Reversible Spells">
  Each time you prepare a reversible spell, choose whether you prepare its primary or reverse form. The chosen form is treated as the prepared spell.

  Preparing one form does not prepare the other. To have both forms available, you must prepare each separately.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Casting Spells">
  You use the Sacred casting method described in the spellcasting chapter. You must present your holy symbol when casting a spell, and the hand used to present it is your Casting Hand. Your Holy Symbol feature explains how you present it.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Cantrips">
  Cantrips are simple prayers that require little devotion to prepare.

  When you prepare your spells as part of a Safe Rest, you may also prepare the number of cantrips shown on your Spell Progression table from the Cleric cantrip list.

  Your Spell Progression table also shows the number of cantrip casts in your shared pool. You regain all expended cantrip casts when you complete a Night's Rest.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>
