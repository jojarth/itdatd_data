Druids are keepers of the old mysteries, preserving the sacred traditions, histories, and wisdom of ages long past. They wander the world in search of forgotten lore, to witness great events worthy of remembrance, and to preserve the proper order of the mortal and spiritual worlds. They serve as priests, judges, philosophers, and teachers, interpreting the will of the natural and spiritual orders alike.

As a druid, you gain the following class features.

<GameplayCard
  color="purple"
  title="The Aspirant's Journey"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Druid Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Druids have a long road ahead of them, and every journey begins as an Aspirant. Upon becoming a Druid, you must devote yourself to one of four philosophical callings: Compassion, Stewardship, Leadership, or Knowledge. Your chosen calling grants you a unique feature and keyword, reflecting the ideals that will guide your journey.

Additionally, the Contemplations granted by your Journey Features possess both passive benefits and Invocations. You can invoke the Invocation benefit of one of your currently selected Contemplations. You can do this once, and regain it's use upon finishing any Night's Rest. 

  <FeatureSection title="Aspirant of Compassion">
    You gain the **Druid of Compassion** keyword.
    
    When you preform the Caregiving downtime activity, characters under your care recover additional grit equal to 1/2 the level of the class that provided this feature.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Aspirant of Stewardship">
    You gain the **Druid of Stewardship** keyword.
    
   When undertaking Downtime Activities relating to stewardship, farming, animal husbandry, brewing, or crafting, gain a +4 bonus to your chance of success.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Aspirant of Leadership">
    You gain the **Druid of Leadership** keyword.
    
    Increase the Loyalty bonus granted to your Henchmen by one-half your Druid Level (minimum of 1).
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Aspirant of Knowledge">
    You gain the **Druid of Knowledge** keyword.
    
    You may perform the Research Downtime Activity while undertaking any other Downtime Activity. 
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  color="purple"
  title="The Worldly Journey"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Druid Feature * Journey Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
The Worldly Journey teaches the truths of mortal existence. Through meditation and study, Druids contemplate the archetypes that shape our lives and our place within the world. Select one Contemplation from the list below and gain its benefits. You retain the benefits of your selected Contemplation until you spend a Downtime Event contemplating another.

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of The Sage">
    You gain a +1 Magic Bonus to Attribute Tests where your Class Skill Bonus would apply.
    
    **Invocation**: You can use this invocation after failing any Attribute Test. You can reroll the failed test, but must use the new result.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of the Mystic">
    While you are Contemplating the Mystic, the Detect Magic spell becomes a Druid spell for you. If you end the Contemplation of the Mystic, any spell slots that held the Detect Magic spell are immediately emptied.
    
    **Invocation**: You can cast the Detect Magic 1st level spell without using a spell slot. When cast this way, the duration is doubled. 
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of The Mother">
    When you complete a Night's rest, recover additional Grit equal to one-half your Druid Level (minimum of 1).

    **Invocation**: You can cast the Cure Wounds 1st level spell without using a spell slot. You can cast the spell this way, even if it is not on your spell list.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of The King">
    Increase your maximum henchmen by 1.

    **Invocation**: When a henchmen that can see or hear you fails a Morale check, you can use a Reaction to allow them to reroll the morale check with a +4 bonus.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of The Heirophant">
    While this Contemplation is active, any ally who can see an hear you that would not normally be able to participate in an Attribute Test can, but they participate with no Skill Bonus. 

    **Invocation**: When an ally that can see and hear you fails a Attribute Test, you can use a Reaction and allow them to reroll the failed test, but must use the new result.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of The Companion">
    You can utilize the Help Activity in combat 1 Phase earlier than normal.

    **Invocation**: You use your Reaction, and allow an ally that can see and hear you to React to something that would normally allow them to React. They can use this feature even if they have acted or Reactied in This Phase, or if they have already used their Reaction.
  </FeatureSection>

</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  color="purple"
  title="Spellcasting"
  subeyebrow="2nd Level Druid Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Through ancient rites and forgotten mysteries, you have learned to prepare miracles within your mind. Each miracle is an ancient mystery that can be held only temporarily. When invoked, that mystery is expended and slips from memory until it is prepared again through meditation and ritual.

A Druid's spells are considered clerical spells.
  
  <FeatureSection title="Spell Preparation">
  Immediately after completing a Safe Rest, you may perform a personal ritual to prepare your spells. During this ritual, you offer prayers to a higher power, beseeching it to grant you sacred miracles. Each prepared spell occupies one spell slot of its level. Druids may prepare any spell on the Druid spell list, so long as they have the correct level of spell slot. 

  For example, a 4th-level Druid prepares three 1st-level spells and two 2nd-level spells. Preparing the same spell multiple times allows you to cast it multiple times before resting again. Prepared spells remain in memory until cast.
  
  Spell Preparation can only be done immediately following a Safe Rest.

  </FeatureSection>
  
  <FeatureSection title="Casting Spells">
  To cast a prepared spell, you must:
  
  * Have the spell prepared.
  * Both hands must be free to gesture.
  * Be able to speak clearly.
 
  Some spells also require material components. If so, the required component is used in place of your holy symbol when casting the spell.
  
  After resolving the spell's effects, the sacred miracle fades from your memory. The spell slot becomes empty and cannot be used again until you prepare spells after completing another Safe Rest.
  </FeatureSection>
  
  <FeatureSection title="Cantrips">
  Cantrips are simple prayers that require little devotion to prepare.

  Whenever you prepare your spells after completing a Safe Rest, you also prepare a number of cantrips from the Druid cantrip list. The Spell Progression table shows how many cantrips you may prepare, as well as the number of cantrip casts available in your shared pool.

  Each time you cast a prepared cantrip, you expend one available cantrip cast from your shared pool, regardless of which prepared cantrip you cast. Unlike leveled spells, cantrips are not erased from your memory when cast and remain prepared until you prepare your spells again.

  You regain all expended cantrip casts whenever you complete a night's rest.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard

  title="The Inner Journey"
  subeyebrow="5th Level Druid Feature * Journey Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
The Inner Journey teaches that wisdom is not freely given, but earned through sacrifice, change, and understanding. Select one Contemplation from the list below and gain its benefits. You retain the benefits of your selected Contemplation until you spend a Downtime Event contemplating another.

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of The Traveller">
    Increase your base Move Speed by +5 feet.

    **Invocation**: You can invoke this feature when you perform any movement, and that movement does not provoke Opportunity Actions. 
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of The Warrior">
    You gain a +1 Magic Bonus to Attack Rolls.

    **Invocation**: You can use this Invocation after missing with an Attack Roll, you can reroll the attack roll, but must use the new result.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of The Guide">
    You cannot become lost by non-magical mean while travelling. 

    **Invocation**: After invoking this feature, you can ignore the effects of difficult terrain for 1 minute.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of The Turning">
    If you are suffering any penalties for Attribute Tests, you can select one penalty source and ignore it. This feature does not work on sources that have a -4 penalty or larger.  

    **Invocation**: You can invoke this feature, and spend 1 minute in quiet contemplation, afterwards you can swap any one of your prepared spells with another on your spell list of the appropriate level. If this feature is interuppted, you do not use your invocation. 
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of The Judge">
    While you are Contemplating the Judge, the Command spell becomes a Druid spell for you. If you end the Contemplation of the Judge, any spell slots that held the Judge spell are immediately emptied. 

    **Invocation**: You can cast the Command 1st level spell without using a spell slot. When cast using this feature, creatures have a -2 Magic Penalty to their saving throws. 
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of The Hanged Man">
    When you make an Attribute Test, you can grant yourself a penalty between -1 and -5 to the test. If the test is successful, the next attribute test you make gains a bonus equal to the penalty you gave yourself +1. 

    **Invocation**: If you are suffering from a temporary effect causing the Blinded, Dazed, Dazzled, or Deafend, you can invoke this feature and end the condition. 
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of Death">
    While contemplating Death, you may change which cantrips you have prepared whenever you complete a Night's Rest, rather than only after a Safe Rest.
    
    **Invocation**: When you make a Saving Throw against an effect that would impose a condition but would not kill you outright, you may invoke death and choose to automatically fail. If you do, the condition lasts for only 1 round.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Contemplation of The Harmonious">
    While contemplating harmoy, you can cast the reverse of any spell you have prepared without needing to prepare it separately. 

    **Invocation**: You may use this invocation before making any d20 test. You treat the test as if you rolled a 10.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>
