Fighters meet danger head-on. Whether they are disciplined soldiers, hardened mercenaries, daring duelists, or stubborn village champions, they rely on mastery of arms and armor to hold the line, protect their companions, and prevail through endurance.

## Fighter Gameplay
Below is a list of information needed to play a fighter at the various levels of play.
<GameplayCard
  colorSet="System"
  title="Requirements and Requisites"
  toc
  tocDepth={3}
>
  <FeatureSection title="Keywords">
    <FeatureSubsection title="Archetype Keywords">
      **Soldier**
    </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Prime Requisite">
    A fighter relies on strength of limb and raw might. Your prime requisite is **Brawn**, and you gain bonus experience points based on this score.

    - If your Prime Requisite is 13 or higher, you gain a +5% bonus to all experience points earned.
    - If your Prime Requisite is 16 or higher, you gain a +10% bonus to all experience points earned.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="System"
  title="Proficiencies"
  toc
  tocDepth={3}
>
  <FeatureSection title="Armor and Shields">
    <FeatureSubsection title="Armor">
      You are proficient with *all* armor.
    </FeatureSubsection>
    <FeatureSubsection title="Shields">
      You are proficient with *all* shields.
    </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Weapons">
    <FeatureSubsection title="Weapon Groups">
      You begin play Familiar with the following weapon groups: *Brawling*, *Daggers*, *Single-Handed Swords*, *Long-Gripped Swords*, *Great Swords*, *Single-Handed Axes*, *Two-Handed Axes*, *Clubs and Maces*, *Hammers and Picks*, *Two-Handed Bludgeons*, *Flails*, *Staves*, *Spears*, *Pikes*, *Mounted Lances*, *Polearms*, *Bows*, *Crossbows*, and *Throwing Darts*.
    </FeatureSubsection>
    <FeatureSubsection title="Weapon Proficiency Points">
      You begin play with **6 Weapon Proficiency Points**. During character creation, you can use these points to upgrade your proficiency with any weapon group or single exotic weapon, up to Proficient. You must spend all 6 points during character creation.

      This total includes the additional points granted by your [Weapon Master](#1st-level-weapon-master) class feature.

      For additional information, see [*Gaining Weapon Proficiency*](/playersguide/02-character_creation/04-math/#gaining-weapon-proficiency) in Chapter 02: Character Creation.
    </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

## Fighter Features
As a fighter, you gain the following class features.

| **Level** | **Features** |
|:---------:|:-------------|
| **1**     | Laden Fighting, Weapon Master |
| **3**     | Unyielding |
| **4**     | Keyword Feature |
| **6**     | Hard-Won Lessons |
| **8**     | Keyword Feature |
| **9**     | Weapon Master (improvement) |
| **10**    | Establishment |
| **11**    | Unyielding (improvement) |

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Laden Fighting"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Fighter Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
When determining how much you can carry, treat your EP breakpoints as if they were 2 points higher.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Weapon Master"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Fighter Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
When determining your Weapon Proficiency Points, you gain an amount equal to three times your Combat Bonus instead of the normal value. You must spend all WPPs at 1st level like normal.

Additionally, you gain a +15% bonus to success chances when undertaking Weapon Training Downtime Events.

  <FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 9th Level">
        * Reduce the Speed Factor of any weapon you wield by 1 (to a minimum of 2) if you are at least Expert with its weapon group.
      </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>


<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Unyielding"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Fighter Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, when you fail a Saving Throw, you can reroll it, but you must use the new result. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a *Meditation Downtime Event*.

  <FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
    <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 11th Level">
      * You can use this feature twice, and you regain both uses when you finish a *Meditation Downtime Event*. You can't use it more than once on the same Saving Throw.
    </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>
