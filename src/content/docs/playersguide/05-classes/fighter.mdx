The fighter class is a broad classification of those who make their mark on the world through strength of arms and armor. They are often found on the front lines, defending others to allow them to preform their duty. 

## Fighter Gameplay
Below is a list of information needed to play a fighter at the various levels of play.

## Fighter Features
As a fighter, you gain the following class features.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Laden Fighting"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Fighter Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
When determining how much you can carry, treat your EP breakpoints as if they were 2 points higher.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Weapon Master"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Fighter Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
When determining your Weapon Proficiency Points, you gain an amount equal to three times your Combat Bonus instead of the normal value. You must spend all WPPs at 1st level like normal.

Additionally, you gain a +15% bonus to success chances when undertaking Weapon Training Downtime Events.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Unyielding"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Fighter Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, after failing a saving throw, you can use this feature and reroll it. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a *Meditation Downtime Event*.
</GameplayCard>
