Jofurr are boar-warriors consecrated to the gods. They stand at the point of the battle wedge beneath helms crowned with the boar, and the holy power that wards them holds firm where other warriors would break.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Svinfylking"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Jofurr Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
As an activity costing 2 Actions, starting in Phase 2, you can enter a battle trance called the Svinfylking. You gain the Battle Trance condition for as long as your Svinfylking lasts. You cannot enter your Svinfylking while under the Slow or Sluggish conditions, although gaining those conditions while in your Svinfylking will not cause it to end. 

<FeatureSection title="Battle Trance Effects">
  * You can move through a space that a foe occupies, so long as you do not end your movement there.
  * When a foe uses Shield Block against your melee attack, increase its shield's Break Chance by 2.
  * When you hit a foe wielding a shield with a melee attack and it does not Shield Block, it must still make a Break Roll for its shield, as you batter its defenses aside. Your increased Break Chance does not apply to this roll.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Adjacent Allies">
  * Each round you maintain your Svinfylking, choose one ally adjacent to you. That ally gains the benefits of your Svinfylking for 1 round, but is not considered to be under a Battle Trance.
  * If the chosen ally is also in their own Svinfylking, when a foe uses Shield Block against either of your melee attacks, increase its shield's Break Chance by an additional 1.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Maintaining the Battle Trance">
  * Each round after the round you enter it, you must spend 1 Action to maintain your Svinfylking.
  * If you have not maintained your Svinfylking by the end of Phase 9, it ends.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Ending the Battle Trance">
  * Your Svinfylking lasts a number of rounds equal to your Presence score. You can end it early at any time (no Action required).
  * When your Svinfylking ends for any reason, you are Slow (2) until the end of the following round.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 5th Level">
    * When a foe's shield makes a Break Roll because it did not Shield Block against your melee attack, increase its Break Chance by 1.
  </FeatureSubsection>
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 9th Level">
    * If you use a Master Action to make a melee weapon attack, or take any damage, during a round, your Svinfylking is maintained for that round without spending an Action. 
  </FeatureSubsection>
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 13th Level">
    * When your Svinfylking ends, you are Slow (1) instead of Slow (2).
  </FeatureSubsection>
</FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Tusk Gore"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Jofurr Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, while you are under any Battle Trance, if you make a melee attack in a Phase immediately after a Phase in which you moved at least 10 feet toward your target, increase your weapon's damage dice by 1 dice step. If you make the attack with an Unarmed Strike, it deals 1d4 damage instead of its normal damage.
</GameplayCard>

  title="Boar's Strike"
  subeyebrow="5th Level Jofurr Feature"

Upon reaching 5th level, while you are under any Battle Trance, all melee weapons you wield are treated as if they had the Engaging feature. You must be at least Familiar with the weapon to do so.

If the weapon already has the Engaging feature, you can use its movement even if you begin in or enter difficult terrain.
</GameplayCard>
