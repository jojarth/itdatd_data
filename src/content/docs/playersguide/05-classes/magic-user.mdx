Magic-Users pursue magic as a discipline of study, experiment, and hard-won insight. Their spellbooks turn knowledge into possibility: with planning and preparation, no other class commands a broader range of magical solutions.

## Magic-User Features
As a magic-user, you gain the following class features.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="System"
  title="Spellcasting"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Magic-User Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Through years of study, you have learned to record magical formulae within your spell books and commit them to memory. Each formula can be held within your mind only temporarily. When cast, its pattern collapses and is forgotten until prepared again.

Your spells are **magical spells**, and you use the **Free-Hand** casting method. You otherwise follow the general rules in [Chapter 09: Spellcasting](/playersguide/09-spellcasting/).

  <FeatureSection title="Spell Books">
  Your spell books contain the magical formulae you have researched and mastered. You can prepare only spells recorded within one of your spell books.

  You must have access to one of your spell books whenever you prepare spells, but never when casting them. Because every Magic-User develops their own notation and theories, you cannot prepare spells directly from another Magic-User's spell book.

  Many Magic-Users maintain several spell books, carrying only those needed for a particular journey. If you have no spell book available, the only spell you may prepare is Read Magic.

  You may discover spell books, scrolls, or other magical writings during your adventures. These cannot be prepared directly. You must first decipher them using Read Magic, then spend the required time and expense copying the spell into one of your own spell books.

    <div class="yellow-table compact-table">
    | **Spell Level** | **Cost** | **Time** |
    |:--:|:--:|:--:|
    | **1st** | 50 Silver | 2 days |
    | **2nd** | 1 Gold | 4 days |
    | **3rd** | 10 Gold | 6 days |
    | **4th** | 25 Gold | 8 days |
    | **5th** | 100 Gold | 10 days |
    | **6th** | 500 Gold | 20 days |
    </div>

  More information on spell books can be found in the equipment chapter.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Spell Preparation">
  As part of a Safe Rest, you may spend one uninterrupted hour studying your spell books. During this time, you may prepare any spells recorded within them for which you have spell slots of the appropriate levels.

  The spells you prepare take effect when you complete the Safe Rest. If the Safe Rest is not completed, the spells are not prepared.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Reversible Spells">
  When you prepare a reversible spell, you prepare the spell itself rather than choosing one of its forms. Each time you cast that preparation, choose whether to cast its primary or reverse form.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Casting Spells">
  You use the Free-Hand casting method described in the spellcasting chapter. Your Casting Hand must be free to perform the spell's Components.

  In addition, you cannot cast spells while wearing armor or wielding a shield.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Cantrips">
  Cantrips are minor magical formulae that occupy little space within your mind.

  By spending one uninterrupted hour studying your spell books, you may prepare the number of cantrips shown on your Spell Progression table from among those recorded within them. Unlike preparing non-cantrip spells, you do not need to complete a Safe Rest before preparing your cantrips.

  Your Spell Progression table also shows the number of cantrip casts in your shared pool. You regain all expended cantrip casts when you complete a Night's Rest.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="choicefeature"
  title="Signature Spell"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Magic-User Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, select one 1st-level spell recorded in your spell books that you have cast at least once during play. You may prepare this spell even when you do not have access to a spell book.

You gain one additional 1st-level spell slot. This slot may only be used to prepare the chosen spell.
</GameplayCard>
