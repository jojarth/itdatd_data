Magic-Users are characters who specialize in the casting of magical spells. Playing a Magic-User gives you access to more magical power than any other class.

## Magic-User Gameplay
Below is a list of information needed to play a magic-user at the various levels of play.
<GameplayCard
  type="class"

>
  <FeatureSection title="Prime Requisite">
    Your prime requsite is logic, and you gain bonus experience points based on your logic score.
    - If your logic score is 13 or higher, you gain a +5% bonus to all experience points earned.
    - If your logic score is 16 or higher, you gain a +10% bonus to all experience points earned.
  </FeatureSection>
  </GameplayCard>

    You are proficient with ***light armor***.
  </FeatureSection>
  
  <FeatureSection title="Shield Proficiency">
    You are proficient with ***bucklers*** and ***small shields***.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Weapon Proficiency">
    You begin play familiar with ***crossbows, light blades***, and ***staves***, and start with 2 weapon proficiency points that you can use to increase your proficiency level with any weapon, to a maximum level of proficient.
  </FeatureSection>

## Magic-User Features
As a magic-user, you gain the following class features.

<GameplayCard
  type="feature"
  title="Spellcasting"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Magic-User Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
 You have learned shape reality through the study of ancient formulae recorded within your spell books. Through meditation, preparation, and careful casting, you can bind these formulae within your mind and release them when needed.
 
  <FeatureSection title="Meditation Ritual">
  You may perform a Meditation Ritual, a process of quiet study and contemplation that requires one uninterrupted hour. During a Meditation Ritual, you may exchange any prepared cantrips for others recorded in one of your spell books that you have access to. Some of your class features require this meditation ritual.
  
  A Meditation Ritual alone cannot prepare spells. Spells may only be prepared following a Safe Rest.
  </FeatureSection>
  
  <FeatureSection title="Spell Books">
  A spell book is a collection of notes that you have researched and created. Your spell book is required to prepare, but not cast, spells. Your spell book is a collection of notes that only you can understand. Since the casting of magical spells is so complex, requiring seemingly unrelated things to be in consideration to prepare spells, you cannot prepare spells from any other magic-user's spell books.
  
  Many magic-users will elect to create multiple spell books, and keep only one or two of them on them at a time. With some exceptions, without your spell book you can only prepare the Read Magic spell.
  
  Magic-Users are incredible possessive about their spell books, as well as the spells contained within, outside of master\apprentice relationships, most magic-users will not share spells inside their spellbooks without a considerable up-front fee.
  
  As you adventure, you may obtain defeated foes spell books, or find scrolls. You cannot prepare from these, but can utilize the Read Magic spell to unlock the ability to transcribe notes found within into your own spell book. Aside from the cost of the Read Magic spell, or fees another spell caster would charge you to access thier spell book, it costs a great deal of research and time to transcribe spells into your spell book.

  <FeatureSection title="Spell Preparation">
  In order to preform spell preparation as a magic-user, you must preform your meditation ritual shortly after waking from a Safe Rest. During this time, you pour over your spell book, reading the formulae and commiting the magic to memory.
  
  You prepare each spell into an individual spell slot of it's level. For example, at 3rd level you can prepare a total of 5 spells: three 1st-level spells, and two 2nd-level spells. If you think you will need to cast the same spell more than one time during your adventure, you may wish to prepare it more than once, and into multiple slots.
  
  Spell Preparation can only be done immediately following a Safe Rest.

  :::tip[Spellcasting in Cerrix]{icon="information"}
  It is by design that spell casters prepare their spells once per adventure, not per day (with the exception of minor spells, such as Cantrips).
  :::
  </FeatureSection>
  
  <FeatureSection title="Casting Spells">
  In order to cast a magic-user spell, you must have the spell prepared, you must have at least one hand free, be able to speak in a clear and consice voice, and not be wearing any armor or wielding any shield.
  
  Many spells also require a material component. The material component must be provided when casting a spell. When a spell requires a material component, it must be held in the hand normally free for spell casting when you cast the spell. Some spells have additional spell casting notes.
  
  Once you cast a spell, the effects listed in the spell's description take place, and the spell is erased from your mind, freeing the spell slot for another spell to be prepared in it.
  </FeatureSection>
  
    <FeatureSection title="Cantrips">
    You have access to specialized spells called Cantrips. Cantrips as minor spells that don't occupy a lot of room in your magical mind, and are not wiped clean after casting them.
    
    When you first gain access to the spellcasting feature, you can prepare a number cantrips out of your spell book (based on your level). Instead of casting a cantrip, and having it wiped from your memeory, you can cast these prepared cantrips a combined number of times based on your level. You recover all expended uses of your cantrips upon finishing any rest.
