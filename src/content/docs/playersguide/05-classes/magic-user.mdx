Magic-Users are characters who specialize in the casting of magical spells. Playing a Magic-User gives you access to more magical power than any other class.

## Magic-User Gameplay
Below is a list of information needed to play a magic-user at the various levels of play.
<GameplayCard
  type="class"
  title="Requirements and Requisites"
  toc
  tocDepth={3}
>
  <FeatureSection title="Class Keywords">
  As a magic-user, you have the _arcanist_ class keyword.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Prime Requisite">
    Your prime requsite is logic, and you gain bonus experience points based on your logic score.
    - If your logic score is 13 or higher, you gain a +5% bonus to all experience points earned.
    - If your logic score is 16 or higher, you gain a +10% bonus to all experience points earned.
  </FeatureSection>
  </GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  type="class"
  title="Proficiencies"
  toc
  tocDepth={3}
>
  <FeatureSection title="Armor Proficiency">
    You are proficient with ***light armor***.
  </FeatureSection>
  
  <FeatureSection title="Shield Proficiency">
    You are proficient with ***bucklers*** and ***small shields***.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Weapon Proficiency">
  You begin play Familiar with **crossbows, light blades,** and **staves**.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

## Magic-User Features
As a magic-user, you gain the following class features.

<GameplayCard
  type="feature"
  title="Spellcasting"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Magic-User Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Through years of study, you have learned to record magical formulae within your spell books and commit them to memory. Each spell is an intricate pattern that can be held within the mind only temporarily. When cast, the pattern collapses and is forgotten until it is prepared again.

  <FeatureSection title="Meditation Ritual">
  You may perform a Meditation Ritual, which requires one uninterrupted hour of quiet study.
  
  During a Meditation Ritual, you may replace any prepared cantrips with other cantrips recorded in one of your spell books. Some class features also require you to complete a Meditation Ritual.
  
  A Meditation Ritual does not prepare spells. Spells may only be prepared immediately after completing a Safe Rest.  
  </FeatureSection>
  
  <FeatureSection title="Spell Books">
  Your spell book contains the magical formulae you have researched and mastered.
  
  You must have access to one of your spell books whenever you prepare spells, but never when casting them. Because every magic-user develops their own notation and theories, you cannot prepare spells directly from another magic-user's spell book.
  
  Many magic-users maintain several spell books, carrying only those needed for a particular journey. If you have no spell book available, the only spell you may prepare is Read Magic.
  
  Spell books are among a magic-user's most valuable possessions. Outside of trusted master-apprentice relationships, few are willing to allow another to study their work without substantial payment.
  
  You may discover spell books, scrolls, or other magical writings during your adventures. These cannot be prepared directly. Instead, you must first decipher them using Read Magic, then spend the required time and expense copying the spell into one of your own spell books.

  <FeatureSection title="Spell Preparation">
  Immediately after completing a Safe Rest, you may perform a Meditation Ritual to prepare your spells. During this time, you study your spell book and commit individual spells to memory. Each prepared spell occupies one spell slot of its level.
  
  For example, a 3rd-level Magic-User prepares three 1st-level spells and two 2nd-level spells. Preparing the same spell multiple times allows you to cast it multiple times before resting again. Prepared spells remain in memory until cast.
  
  Spell Preparation can only be done immediately following a Safe Rest.

  :::tip[Spellcasting in Cerrix]{icon="information"}
  It is by design that spell casters prepare their spells once per adventure, not per day (with the exception of minor spells, such as Cantrips).
  :::
  </FeatureSection>
  
  <FeatureSection title="Casting Spells">
  To cast a prepared spell, you must:
  
  * Have the spell prepared.
  * Have at least one free hand.
  * Be able to speak clearly.
  * Not be wearing armor or wielding a shield.
  
  Some spells also require material components. If so, the required component must be held in your free hand when the spell is cast.
  
  After resolving the spell's effects, the magical formula is erased from your memory. The spell slot becomes empty and cannot be used again until you prepare spells after another Safe Rest.
  </FeatureSection>
  
  <FeatureSection title="Cantrips">
  Cantrips are simple magical formulae that occupy little space within your mind.
  
  Whenever you perform a Meditation Ritual, choose a number of cantrips from among those recorded in your spell books, as shown in the table below.
  
  You may cast your prepared cantrips a combined number of times before you must rest. Each time you cast any prepared cantrip, you expend one cantrip use from your shared pool, regardless of which cantrip you cast. You regain all expended cantrip uses whenever you complete a night's rest.

<GameplayCard
  type="feature"
  title="Signature Spell"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Magic-User Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, select one 1st-level spell recorded in your spell books that you have cast at least once during play. You may prepare this spell even when you do not have access to a spell book.

You gain one additional 1st-level spell slot. This slot may only be used to prepare the chosen spell.
</GameplayCard>
