Paladins are champions of the faith who stand at the forefront of every righteous struggle. Tempered by martial discipline and guided by unwavering conviction, they wield both steel and sacred power against the enemies of their creed.

## Paladin Gameplay
Below is a list of information needed to play a Paladin at the various levels of play.
<GameplayCard
  color="green"
  title="Requirements and Requisites"
  toc
  tocDepth={3}
>
    <FeatureSection title="Class Requirements">
    To become a Paladin, you must meet the following attribute requirements:
   
    - Your Brawn score must be 13 or higher.
    - Your Heartiness score must be 11 or higher.
    - Your Logic score must be 9 or higher.
    - Your Reason score must be 13 or higher.
    - Your Savvy score must be 11 or higher.
    - Your Presence score must be 13 or higher.
   
    These demanding requirements reflect the exceptional character expected of a paladin. Few possess the strength, resolve, wisdom, and force of personality required to uphold the sacred oath.
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Class Keywords">
        **Priest**, **Soldier**
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Prime Requisite">
        Your prime requsites are brawn, reason, and presence, and you gain bonus experience points based on these scores.
        - If your brawn, reason, and presence scores are 13 or higher, you gain a +5% bonus to all experience points earned.
        - If your brawn, reason, and presence scores are 16 or higher, you gain a +10% bonus to all experience points earned.
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Multi-Classing">
        As a Paladin, your path is one of strict adherance, and as such you cannot be a multi-class character, even if you meet all other prerequsites. 
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Paladin Tenents">
        A Paladin is expected to uphold the following tenets:
        - **Honor the Law**. Respect just authority, keep your word, and uphold the lawful customs of the lands you travel whenever doing so does not conflict with your other tenets.
        - **Protect the Innocent**. Defend those who cannot defend themselves and stand against cruelty, tyranny, and needless suffering.
        - **Act with Honor**. Show courage, mercy, and integrity in both victory and defeat. Do not lie, betray those under your protection, or resort to dishonorable means when an honorable path remains.
        - **Serve the Greater Good**. When your tenets come into conflict, the protection of innocent life and the cause of righteousness take precedence over obedience to law.

        ###### Paladin Keyword
        You have the **Paladin** keyword.

        ###### Breaking your Oath
        A Paladin who willfully and knowingly violates the tenets of their oath immediately loses the benefits of any class feature marked Oath-Dependent. While fallen, you are treated as though you do not possess the **Paladin** or **Priest** keywords for the purpose of meeting prerequisites, selecting character features, and using abilities.

        A fallen Paladin may seek redemption through sincere repentance and by completing whatever penance the Game Master determines is appropriate. Once the Paladin has atoned for their transgression, these class features are immediately restored.

        If you continue to adventure while fallen, you continue to gain experience as a Paladin normally. However, you do not gain the benefits of any oath-dependent class features you would otherwise acquire while fallen. If your paladinhood is restored, you immediately regain all lost class features, including any you would have gained by advancing in level during that time.
    </FeatureSection>
  </GameplayCard>

## Paladin Features
As a Paladin, you gain the following class features.

<GameplayCard
  color="purple"
  title="Holy Symbol"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Paladin Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Many of your class features require you to present your holy symbol. A holy symbol is a physical representation of your faith and devotion, and may take nearly any form, such as an amulet, prayer beads, a reliquary, or another relic sacred to your beliefs.

To present your holy symbol, you must hold it aloft in clear view. This requires a free hand. Alternatively, you may emblazon your holy symbol upon a shield. Presenting such a shield fulfills any requirement to present your holy symbol.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  color="purple"
  title="Lay on Hands"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Paladin Feature • Oath-Dependent"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
You can use this feature to cast the *Minor Healing* cantrip without expending a Cantrip use, even if it is not prepared. When cast in this way, the cantrip restores Grit equal to three times your Paladin level instead of its normal amount.

Once you use this feature, you must complete a Night's Rest before you can use it again.

<FeatureSection title="11th Level Improvement">
Starting at 11th level, when you use this feature, you may choose to remove one disease affecting the target. If you do, the Minor Healing cantrip restores its normal amount of Grit rather than three times your Paladin level.
</FeatureSection>

</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  color="purple"
  title="Turn Undead"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Paladin Feature * Oath-Dependent"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, Paladins can channel sacred power to drive back the undead. To use this ability, you must openly present a symbol of your faith. **Turn Undead** requires **1 Master Action** and may be performed beginning in **Phase 1**. You must present your holy symbol to turn undead. 

  <FeatureSection title="Determine Highest Level">
    When you attempt to Turn Undead, first determine the highest-level undead creature you can affect.

    Roll **1d20** and compare the result to your **Presence** Attribute using the table below. The result determines the highest creature level that can be affected by this attempt.

    Undead creatures **2 or more levels higher** than your Paladin Level cannot be affected. Likewise, undead creatures **5 or more levels lower** than your Paladin Level are always affected, regardless of your roll.

    <div class="yellow-table">
    |        **d20 Result**        | **Highest Creature Level** |
    | :-------------------------- | :------------------------: |
    | **Presence +6** | Paladin Level -4 |
    | **Presence +3** | Paladin Level -3 |
    | **Presence**    | Paladin Level -2  |
    | **Presence -3** | Paladin Level - 1 |
    | **Presence -6** | Paladin Level     |
    | **Presence -9** | Paladin Level +1  |
    </div>
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Determine Turning Capacity">
    Next, determine the total number of creature levels you can affect. Roll 2d6 and add half your Paladin Level (rounded down), then subtract 1. The result is your Turning Capacity.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Turn Undead Effect">
    Beginning with the lowest-level Eligible Creature within **60 feet** that can see you, subtract each affected creature's level from your Turning Capacity until you either have insufficient capacity to affect another creature or there are no more eligible creatures.

    Affected creatures immediately flee from you by the safest available route. Instead of fleeing, creatures that are **7 or more levels lower** than your Paladin Level are instantly destroyed, crumbling to dust.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Creature Interactions"> 
  * Creatures with the **Necros** keyword are Eligible Creatures.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  color="purple"
  title="Aura of Power"
  subeyebrow="6th Level Paladin Feature • Oath-Dependent"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 6th level, you radiate an aura of divine power in a 10-foot emanation.

Allies within your aura gain a +1 magic bonus to Saving Throws. Hostile creatures within your aura instead suffer a -1 magic penalty to Saving Throws.

In addition, hostile creatures within your aura reduce the damage dice of their weapon attacks by 1 dice step if they attack a creature other than you. 

Your aura is always active and cannot be suppressed. However, it ceases to function while you are unconscious.

  <FeatureSection title="Creature Interactions">
    Creatures with the **Aeon** keyword are unaffected by your aura.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  color="purple"
  title="Spellcasting"
  subeyebrow="7th Level Paladin Feature * Oath-Dependent"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reachin 7th level, and through prayer and unwavering faith, you have learned to prepare sacred miracles within your mind. Each miracle is a sacred blessing that can be held only temporarily. When invoked, that blessing is expended and slips from memory until it is prepared again through prayer.

A Paladin's spells are considered clerical spells. 
  
  <FeatureSection title="Spell Preparation">
  Immediately after completing a Safe Rest, you may perform a personal ritual to prepare your spells. During this ritual, you offer prayers to a higher power, beseeching it to grant you sacred miracles. Each prepared spell occupies one spell slot of its level. Paladins may prepare any spell on the Paladin spell list, so long as they have the correct level of spell slot. 

  For example, a 10th-level Paladin prepares two 1st-level spells and one 2nd-level spell. Preparing the same spell multiple times allows you to cast it multiple times before resting again. Prepared spells remain in memory until cast.
  
  Spell Preparation can only be done immediately following a Safe Rest.

  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Reversible Spells">
  Some Paladin spells have a reverse form. When preparing your spells, you may prepare only the primary form of a spell. You cannot prepare the reverse form, even if it appears on the Paladin spell list.
  </FeatureSection>
  
  <FeatureSection title="Casting Spells">
  To cast a prepared spell, you must:
  
  * Have the spell prepared.
  * Be able to present your holy symbol.
  * Be able to speak clearly.
 
  Some spells also require material components. If so, the required component is used in place of your holy symbol when casting the spell.
  
  After resolving the spell's effects, the sacred miracle fades from your memory. The spell slot becomes empty and cannot be used again until you prepare spells after completing another Safe Rest.
  </FeatureSection>
  
  <FeatureSection title="Cantrips">
  Cantrips are simple prayers that require little devotion to prepare.

  Whenever you prepare your spells after completing a Safe Rest, you also prepare a number of cantrips from the Paladin cantrip list. The Spell Progression table shows how many cantrips you may prepare, as well as the number of cantrip casts available in your shared pool. 

  You always have the Minor Healing cantrip prepared. It does not count against the number of cantrips you can prepare.

  Each time you cast a prepared cantrip, you expend one available cantrip cast from your shared pool, regardless of which prepared cantrip you cast. Unlike leveled spells, cantrips are not erased from your memory when cast and remain prepared until you prepare your spells again.

  You regain all expended cantrip casts whenever you complete a night's rest.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>
