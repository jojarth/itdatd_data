Paladins are oath-bound champions whose faith is expressed through action. They stand where the struggle is hardest, pairing martial discipline with sacred power to defend the innocent, confront evil, and live up to the promises that define them.

## Paladin Gameplay
Below is a list of information needed to play a Paladin at the various levels of play.

    <FeatureSubsection title="Minimum Attributes">
      Few possess the strength, resolve, wisdom, and force of personality the sacred oath demands. To become a Paladin, each of your attribute scores must meet the minimum shown below.

      <div class="green-table compact-table">
      | Brawn | Heartiness | Logic | Reason | Savvy | Presence |

    <FeatureSubsection title="Multi-Classing">
      Your path is one of strict adherence. You cannot be a multi-class character, even if you meet every other prerequisite.
    </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Keywords">
    <FeatureSubsection title="Archetype Keywords">
      **Priest**, **Soldier**
    </FeatureSubsection>
    <FeatureSubsection title="Story Keywords">
      **Paladin**, **Hallowed**

      Your choice of Order also grants you a Story Keyword. See [Order](#1st-level-order).
    </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Prime Requisite">
    A paladin relies on strength of arm, clarity of mind, and a presence that inspires others to follow. Your prime requisites are **Brawn**, **Reason**, and **Presence**, and you gain bonus experience points based on these scores.

    - If all of your Prime Requisites are 13 or higher, you gain a +5% bonus to all experience points earned.
    - If all of your Prime Requisites are 16 or higher, you gain a +10% bonus to all experience points earned.
  </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Paladin Tenets">
        A Paladin is expected to uphold the following tenets:
        - **Honor the Law**. Respect just authority, keep your word, and uphold the lawful customs of the lands you travel whenever doing so does not conflict with your other tenets.
        - **Protect the Innocent**. Defend those who cannot defend themselves and stand against cruelty, tyranny, and needless suffering.
        - **Act with Honor**. Show courage, mercy, and integrity in both victory and defeat. Do not lie, betray those under your protection, or resort to dishonorable means when an honorable path remains.
        - **Serve the Greater Good**. When your tenets come into conflict, the protection of innocent life and the cause of righteousness take precedence over obedience to law.

        <FeatureSubsection title="Breaking your Oath">
        A Paladin who willfully and knowingly violates the tenets of their oath immediately loses the benefits of any class feature marked Oath-Dependent. While fallen, you are treated as though you do not possess the **Paladin** keyword for the purpose of meeting prerequisites, selecting character features, and using abilities. Additionally, you lose access to the **Hallowed** keyword.

        A fallen Paladin may seek redemption through sincere repentance and by completing whatever penance the Game Master determines is appropriate. Once the Paladin has atoned for their transgression, these class features are immediately restored, and they gain the **Paladin** keyword again. However, once fallen, the **Hallowed** keyword cannot be restored. 

        If you continue to adventure while fallen, you continue to gain experience as a Paladin normally. However, you do not gain the benefits of any oath-dependent class features you would otherwise acquire while fallen. If your paladinhood is restored, you immediately regain all lost class features, including any you would have gained by advancing in level during that time.
        </FeatureSubsection>
    </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Chivalric Training"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Paladin Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
When determining your Weapon Proficiency Points, you gain an amount equal to three times your Combat Bonus instead of the normal value. You must spend all WPPs at 1st level like normal.

Additionally, you gain a +15% bonus to success chances when undertaking Weapon Training Downtime Events.
</GameplayCard> 

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Lay on Hands"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Paladin Feature • Oath-Dependent"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
You can use this feature to cast the *Minor Healing* cantrip without expending a Cantrip use, even if it is not prepared. When cast in this way, the cantrip restores Grit equal to three times your Paladin level instead of its normal amount.

Once you use this feature, you must complete a Night's Rest before you can use it again.

  <FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 11th Level">
        * When you use this feature, you may choose to remove one disease affecting the target. If you do, the Minor Healing cantrip restores its normal amount of Grit rather than the Grit provided by this feature.
      </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>

</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Righteous Smite"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Paladin Feature • Oath-Dependent"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
When you deal damage with a weapon attack against a Valid Creature, you deal an extra dice of damage. 

<FeatureSection title="Spell Interactions">
Some Paladin spells are cast in conjunction with utilizing this feature. When they do so, you must announce that you are casting the spell before making the attack roll. If your attack roll is unsuccessful, your spell is still expended. However, these spells are often powerful, and do not allow for a Saving Throw.  
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Creature Interactions">
    <FeatureSubsection title="Valid Creatures">
    * A creature with the **Necros** or **Umbra** keyword is a Valid Creature.
    * A creature with the **Defiled** keyword is a Valid Creature.
  </FeatureSubsection>
</FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 4th Level">
        * When you utilize this feature, you also can add 1/2 your Paladin Level to the damage done.
      </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard

  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, when you fail a Saving Throw, you can reroll it, but you must use the new result. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a *Meditation Downtime Event*.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Turn Undead"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Paladin Feature • Oath-Dependent"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, Paladins can channel sacred power to drive back the undead. To use this ability, you must present your holy symbol as described under Devotional Casting in [Chapter 09: Spellcasting](/playersguide/09-spellcasting/). **Turn Undead** requires **1 Master Action** and may be performed beginning in **Phase 1**.

  <FeatureSection title="Determine Highest Level">
    When you attempt to Turn Undead, first determine the highest-level Valid Creature you can affect.

    Roll **1d20** and compare the result to your **Presence** Attribute using the table below. The result determines the highest creature level that can be affected by this attempt.

    Valid Creatures **2 or more levels higher** than your Paladin Level cannot be affected. Likewise, Valid Creatures **5 or more levels lower** than your Paladin Level are always affected, regardless of your roll.

    <div class="yellow-table compact-table">
    |        **d20 Result**        | **Highest Creature Level** |
    | :-------------------------- | :------------------------: |
    | **Presence +6** | Paladin Level -4 |
    | **Presence +3** | Paladin Level -3 |
    | **Presence**    | Paladin Level -2 |
    | **Presence -3** | Paladin Level -1 |
    | **Presence -6** | Paladin Level    |
    | **Presence -9** | Paladin Level +1 |
    </div>
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Determine Turning Capacity">
    Next, determine the total number of creature levels you can affect. Roll **2d6** and add **half your Paladin Level** (rounded down), then subtract 1. The result is your **Turning Capacity**.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Turn Undead Effect">
    Beginning with the lowest-level Valid Creature within **60 feet** that can see you, subtract each affected creature's level from your Turning Capacity until you either have insufficient capacity to affect another creature or there are no more Valid Creatures. 

    Affected creatures immediately flee from you by the safest available route. Instead of fleeing, creatures that are **7 or more levels lower** than your Paladin Level are instantly destroyed, crumbling to dust.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Creature Interactions"> 
    <FeatureSubsection title="Valid Creatures">
      * A creature with the **Necros** keyword is a Valid Creature.
    </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Paladin-Specific Notes">
    If an effect modifies or otherwise interacts with Turn Undead but does not specify how it applies to Paladins, it still applies to you. For that effect, treat yourself as a Cleric of your Paladin level minus 2.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Sacred Mount"
  subeyebrow="5th Level Paladin Feature • Oath-Dependent"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 5th level, you can cast the *Find Steed* spell without expending a spell slot, even though it is not on the Paladin spell list and you cannot otherwise cast 3rd-level spells. You still perform the spell's Components.

When you cast it this way, the spell has no set duration. Your steed remains until it is reduced to 0 Hit Points or you dismiss it.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Downtime Event.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Aura of Power"
  subeyebrow="6th Level Paladin Feature • Oath-Dependent"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 6th level, you radiate an aura of divine power in a 10-foot emanation.

Allies within your aura gain a +1 Magic Bonus to Saving Throws. Hostile creatures within your aura instead suffer a -1 Magic Penalty to Saving Throws.

In addition, hostile creatures within your aura reduce the damage dice of their weapon attacks by 1 dice step if they attack a creature other than you. 

Your aura is always active and cannot be suppressed. However, it ceases to function while you are unconscious.

  <FeatureSection title="Creature Interactions">
      <FeatureSubsection title="Invalid Creatures">
      * A creature with the **Aeon** keyword is unaffected by your aura.
    </FeatureSubsection>
    <FeatureSubsection title="Valid Creatures">
      * A steed called by the *Find Steed* spell is affected by your aura, despite having the **Aeon** keyword.
    </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="spellcasting"
  title="Spellcasting"
  subeyebrow="7th Level Paladin Feature • Oath-Dependent"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Through prayer, unwavering faith, and devotion to your oath, you have learned to prepare sacred miracles within your mind. Each miracle is a blessing that can be held only temporarily. When invoked, it is expended and slips from memory until granted again through prayer.

Your spells are **clerical spells**, and you use the **Devotional** casting method. You otherwise follow the general rules in [Chapter 09: Spellcasting](/playersguide/09-spellcasting/).

  <FeatureSection title="Spell Preparation">
  As part of a Safe Rest, you may perform a personal ritual to prepare your spells. During this ritual, you offer prayers to a higher power and beseech it to grant you sacred miracles.

  You may prepare any spell on the Paladin spell list for which you have a spell slot of the appropriate level. The spells you prepare take effect when you complete the Safe Rest. If the Safe Rest is not completed, the spells are not prepared.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Spell Scrolls">
  You can cast a spell from a Spell Scroll if it appears on the Paladin spell list and its level is no higher than the highest-level spell you can cast.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Reversible Spells">
  You may prepare only the primary form of a reversible spell. You cannot prepare or cast its reverse form, even if the reversible spell appears on the Paladin spell list.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Casting Spells">
  You use the Devotional casting method described in [Chapter 09: Spellcasting](/playersguide/09-spellcasting/), including its rules for presenting your holy symbol and using your Casting Hand.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Cantrips">
  Cantrips are simple prayers that require little devotion to prepare.

  When you prepare your spells as part of a Safe Rest, you may also prepare the number of cantrips shown on your Spell Progression table from the Paladin cantrip list.

  You always have the Minor Healing cantrip prepared. It does not count against the number of cantrips you can prepare.

  Your Spell Progression table also shows the number of cantrip casts in your shared pool. You regain all expended cantrip casts when you complete a Night's Rest.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>
