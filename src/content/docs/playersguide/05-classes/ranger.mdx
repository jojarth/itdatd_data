Rangers are the hunters and trackers of the wild places. They read the land, follow trails others cannot see, and learn the habits of the creatures they hunt, until no quarry can hide from them for long.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="choicefeature"
  title="Favored Quarry"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Ranger Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
You have studied one kind of creature until you know its every habit. Choose **Beast**, **Monstrosity**, or **Plant**, or choose the Species keyword of a humanoid people, such as **Giant**, **Orc**, or **Goblin**. Creatures with that keyword are your favored quarry. Once made, this choice can't be changed.

* When you hit your favored quarry with a weapon attack, increase the attack's damage dice by 1 dice step.
* When you track your favored quarry, decrease the Y dice of your X-in-Y test by 1 dice step. If the GM instead calls for an Attribute Test, the Situation Bonus from your Tracker feature increases to +3.

  <FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 9th Level">
        * You have seen it all. Choose a second favored quarry from the same options, or choose **Amorphous** or **Incorporeal**. Creatures with either keyword are your favored quarry.
      </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Tracker"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Ranger Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
A Ranger gains bonuses to track quarry and avoid being tracked. Increase your chance to successfully track creatures by +1-in-Y. If the GM instead calls for an Attribute Test, you gain a +2 Situation Bonus to the attribute being tested.

You also know how to cover your trail. Any creature attempting to track you or your party increases the Y dice of its X-in-Y test by 1 dice step. If the GM instead calls for an Attribute Test, the creature has a -2 Situation Penalty on its test.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Ever Watchful"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Ranger Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, if you are ever caught by Surprise, the one Action you are allowed that round can be a Master Action.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Hide in Naught but Flora"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Ranger Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, Rangers have learned to become nearly undiscoverable while in nature. While other allies can be stealthy and hide behind objects, all a Ranger needs to be able to hide is a thicket or full-cover foliage. Rangers must be wearing light or no armor that is of similar color to the foliage they wish to hide in to perform this action, and must remain stationary once hidden.

This action does not allow a Ranger to disappear into flora, and follows the normal rules of stealth, but allows Rangers to hide in situations that others would not be able to.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Move in Utter Silence"
  subeyebrow="5th Level Ranger Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 5th level, while attempting to be stealthy, a Ranger can move so utterly silently that they make no noise. While other allies can be stealthy and quiet, Rangers make absolutely no perceptible noises. Rangers must be lightly encumbered or less to perform this action, and must be wearing light or no armor.

Rangers who meet these conditions can stealth around while making absolutely no noise, and cannot be discovered by means of noise detection.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Wayfinder"
  subeyebrow="6th Level Ranger Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 6th level, you can lead others safely through the wilds. While you travel through the wilderness with your party, your party cannot become lost unless magic is responsible.

You also know where to find food in the wild. When foraging for food, increase your chance to find food by +1-in-Y. If the GM instead calls for an Attribute Test, you gain a +2 Situation Bonus to the attribute being tested.
</GameplayCard>
