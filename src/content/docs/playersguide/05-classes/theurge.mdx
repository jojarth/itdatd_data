Theurges are devoted servants of a higher power who channel sacred magic through prayer, contemplation, and unwavering faith rather than martial discipline. Many take to the road to recover lost relics, spread the teachings of their faith, witness miracles firsthand, or confront the growing darkness wherever it is found.

## Theurge Features
As a theurge, you gain the following class features.

<GameplayCard
  color="purple"
  title="Holy Symbol"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Theurge Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Many of your class features require you to present your holy symbol. A holy symbol is a physical representation of your faith and devotion, and may take nearly any form, such as an amulet, prayer beads, a reliquary, or another relic sacred to your beliefs.

To present your holy symbol, you must hold it aloft in clear view. This requires a free hand. Alternatively, you may emblazon your holy symbol upon a shield. Presenting such a shield fulfills any requirement to present your holy symbol.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  color="purple"
  title="Spellcasting"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Theurge Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Through prayer and unwavering faith, you have learned to prepare sacred miracles within your mind. Each miracle is a sacred blessing that can be held only temporarily. When invoked, that blessing is expended and slips from memory until it is prepared again through prayer.

A Theurge's spells are considered clerical spells. 
  
    <FeatureSection title="Spell Preparation">
        Immediately after completing a Safe Rest, you may perform a personal ritual to prepare your spells. During this ritual, you offer prayers to a higher power, beseeching it to grant you sacred miracles. Each prepared spell occupies one spell slot of its level. Theurges may prepare any spell on the Theurge spell list, so long as they have the correct level of spell slot. 

        For example, a 3rd-level Theurge prepares three 1st-level spells and two 2nd-level spells. Preparing the same spell multiple times allows you to cast it multiple times before resting again. Prepared spells remain in memory until cast.

        Spell Preparation can only be done immediately following a Safe Rest.

            :::tip[Spellcasting in Cerrix]{icon="information"}
            It is by design that spell casters prepare their spells once per adventure, not per day (with the exception of minor spells, such as Cantrips).
            :::
    </FeatureSection>

    <FeatureSection title="Casting Spells">
        To cast a prepared spell, you must:

        * Have the spell prepared.
        * Be able to present your holy symbol.
        * Be able to speak clearly.

        Some spells also require material components. If so, the required component is used in place of your holy symbol when casting the spell.

        After resolving the spell's effects, the sacred miracle fades from your memory. The spell slot becomes empty and cannot be used again until you prepare spells after completing another Safe Rest.
    </FeatureSection>
  
    <FeatureSection title="Cantrips">
        Cantrips are simple prayers that require little devotion to prepare.

        Whenever you prepare your spells after completing a Safe Rest, you also prepare a number of cantrips from the Theurge cantrip list. The Spell Progression table shows how many cantrips you may prepare, as well as the number of cantrip casts available in your shared pool.

        Each time you cast a prepared cantrip, you expend one available cantrip cast from your shared pool, regardless of which prepared cantrip you cast. Unlike leveled spells, cantrips are not erased from your memory when cast and remain prepared until you prepare your spells again.

        You regain all expended cantrip casts whenever you complete a night's rest.
    </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  color="purple"

Starting at 3rd level, you can spend one uninterrupted hour in prayer to replace a prepared spell with another spell of the same level that restores Grit or allows a target to recover from one or more negative effects.
