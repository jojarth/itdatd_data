Theurges are devoted servants of a higher power, called to work miracles through prayer, contemplation, and steadfast faith. Their chosen order and tradition shape that calling, whether they travel to recover relics, preserve sacred teachings, comfort the suffering, or oppose a spreading darkness.

## Theurge Features
As a theurge, you gain the following class features.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Holy Symbol"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Theurge Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Many of your class features require you to present your holy symbol. A holy symbol is a physical representation of your faith and devotion, and may take nearly any form, such as an amulet, prayer beads, a reliquary, or another relic sacred to your beliefs.

To present your holy symbol, you must hold it aloft in clear view. This requires a free hand. Alternatively, you may emblazon your holy symbol upon a shield. Presenting such a shield fulfills any requirement to present your holy symbol.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="System"
  title="Spellcasting"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Theurge Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Through prayer and unwavering faith, you have learned to prepare sacred miracles within your mind. Each miracle is a blessing that can be held only temporarily. When invoked, it is expended and slips from memory until granted again through prayer.

Your spells are **clerical spells**, and you use the **Sacred** casting method. You otherwise follow the general rules in [Chapter 09: Spellcasting](/playersguide/09-spellcasting/).

  <FeatureSection title="Spell Preparation">
  As part of a Safe Rest, you may perform a personal ritual to prepare your spells. During this ritual, you offer prayers to a higher power and beseech it to grant you sacred miracles.

  You may prepare any spell on the Theurge spell list for which you have a spell slot of the appropriate level. The spells you prepare take effect when you complete the Safe Rest. If the Safe Rest is not completed, the spells are not prepared.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Reversible Spells">
  Each time you prepare a reversible spell, choose whether you prepare its primary or reverse form. The chosen form is treated as the prepared spell.

  Preparing one form does not prepare the other. To have both forms available, you must prepare each separately.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Casting Spells">
  You use the Sacred casting method described in the spellcasting chapter. You must present your holy symbol when casting a spell, and the hand used to present it is your Casting Hand. Your Holy Symbol feature explains how you present it.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Cantrips">
  Cantrips are simple prayers that require little devotion to prepare.

  When you prepare your spells as part of a Safe Rest, you may also prepare the number of cantrips shown on your Spell Progression table from the Theurge cantrip list.

  Your Spell Progression table also shows the number of cantrip casts in your shared pool. You regain all expended cantrip casts when you complete a Night's Rest.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
