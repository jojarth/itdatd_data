Thieves excel where preparation and nerve matter more than brute force. They slip past defenses, uncover what others miss, and strike at the moment an enemy is least prepared—whether the obstacle is a lock, a trap, a guarded secret, or a vulnerable foe.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Backstab"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Thief Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
When you attack with a one-handed melee weapon or a ranged weapon that lacks the Heavy property, you deal an additional 1d4 Ambush damage if either of the following is true:

* You hold Combat Supremacy over the target.
* The target is adjacent to one of your allies.

When making a ranged or thrown attack, the target must also be within 30 feet or the weapon's first range increment, whichever is shorter.

  <FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 5th Level">
        * Increase the additional Ambush damage to **2d4**.
      </FeatureSubsection>
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 10th Level">
        * Increase the additional Ambush damage to **3d4**.
      </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Thief Skills"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Thief Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
As a thief, you are able to ***Pick Locks*** and ***Disarm Fine Traps.***

Additionally, when you make an Attribute Test to accomplish feats of stealth, legerdemain, or infiltration, increase your Skill Bonus by 1 for that test.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Hide in Naught but Shadow"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Thief Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, Thieves have learned to utilize darkness and shadows to their greatest extent. While other allies can be stealthy, and hide behind objects, all a Thief needs to be able to hide is shadows and darkness. Thieves must be wearing light or no armor that is dark in color to perform this action, and must remain stationary once hidden. 

This action does not allow a Thief to disappear into a shadow, and follows the normal rules of stealth, but allows Thieves to hide in situations that others would not be able to. 
</GameplayCard>

    :::tip[Thief Skills]{icon="information"}
    A thief using the Hide in Naught but Shadow, Move in Utter Silence, and Climb Sheer Surfaces features does not automatically succeed at these tests. A GM should still make the call for attribute tests.

    However, this lets thieves perform functions above and beyond what other classes can. For example, a fighter simply cannot hide in a shadow; they must find an object to hide behind. A fighter can move with stealth, but not so silently they make no noise. A fighter can climb a sheer surface, but only if they have a rope or other means of climbing available to them. 

    Effectively, these thief skills allow a thief to perform beyond other classes in stealth and infiltration, but they do not negate the need to test the thief.
    :::

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Magic Item Aptitude"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Thief Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Starting at 3rd level, you learn to decipher and activate magical items beyond the limits of your training. You gain the following benefits:

  * You can use spell scrolls of 1st level or lower. 
  * You are treated as having any ancestry for the purpose of using magical items.

  <FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 5th Level">
        * You can utilize 2nd-level Spell Scrolls.
      </FeatureSubsection>
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 7th Level">
        * You can utilize 3rd-level Spell Scrolls. 
        * You can ignore any keyword requirements to utilize magic items.
      </FeatureSubsection>
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 9th Level">
        * You can utilize 4th-level Spell Scrolls.
      </FeatureSubsection>
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 11th Level">
        * You can utilize 5th-level Spell Scrolls. 
        * You immediately recognize when an item is cursed before its effect takes hold. You may choose to stop interacting with the item before the curse applies.
      </FeatureSubsection>
      <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 13th Level">
        * You can utilize 6th-level Spell Scrolls.
      </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Move in Utter Silence"
  subeyebrow="5th Level Thief Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 5th level, while attempting to be stealthy, a Thief can move so utterly silently that they make no noise. While other allies can be stealthy and quiet, Thieves make absolutely no perceptible noises. Thieves must be lightly encumbered or less to perform this action, and must be wearing light or no armor.

Thieves who meet these conditions can stealth around while making absolutely no noise, and cannot be discovered by means of noise detection.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Climb Sheer Surfaces"
  subeyebrow="7th Level Thief Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 7th level, a Thief can climb upon surfaces that seemingly have no handholds. While other allies may be able to scale walls with good handholds or ropes, Thieves need no such things to be able to scale a wall. Thieves must be wearing light or no armor, and be lightly encumbered or less to perform this action. 

Thieves who meet these conditions can climb any walls. They must follow all other rules for climbing, but are able to scale surfaces thought to be impossible to scale unless by magic.
</GameplayCard>
