Ulfhednar are wolf-warriors who hunt in packs. Clad in the skins of wolves, they raid and skirmish, strike from ambush, and run down their enemies with a wolf's speed and cunning.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Pack Fury"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Ulfhednar Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
As an activity costing 2 Actions, starting in Phase 2, you can enter a Pack Fury. You gain the Battle Trance condition for as long as your Pack Fury lasts. You cannot enter your Pack Fury while you are Slow or Sluggish, although gaining either condition while in your Pack Fury does not cause it to end.

<FeatureSection title="Battle Trance Effects">
  * Increase your Move Speed by 5 feet.
  * When you enter your Pack Fury, choose one ally within 30 feet who can see or hear you to be your Packmate. Each round you maintain your Pack Fury, you can choose a new Packmate the same way. Your Packmate remains your Packmate until you choose a new one or your Pack Fury ends.
  * Your Packmate also increases their Move Speed by 5 feet, but is not considered to be under a Battle Trance.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Maintaining the Battle Trance">
  * Each round after the round you enter it, you must spend 1 Action to maintain your Pack Fury.
  * If you have not maintained your Pack Fury by the end of Phase 9, it ends.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Ending the Battle Trance">
  * Your Pack Fury lasts a number of rounds equal to your Presence score. You can end it early at any time (no Action required).
  * When your Pack Fury ends for any reason, you are Slow (2) until the end of the following round.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 5th Level">
    * When you or a Packmate makes a melee weapon attack against a foe that another of you is adjacent to, increase the attack's damage dice by 1 dice step.
  </FeatureSubsection>
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 9th Level">
    * If you or a Packmate holds Combat Supremacy against a foe, the rest of you also hold Combat Supremacy against that foe.
    * If you use a Master Action to make a melee weapon attack, or take any damage, during a round, your Pack Fury is maintained for that round without spending an Action.
  </FeatureSubsection>
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 13th Level">
    * You can have up to two Packmates at a time. When you choose a Packmate, you can choose up to two allies, and each round you maintain your Pack Fury, you can replace either or both of them.
    * When your Pack Fury ends, you are Slow (1) instead of Slow (2).
  </FeatureSubsection>
</FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>
