## Coinage

The common currencies are Copper, Silver, and Gold.

- 100 Copper equals 1 Silver.
- 100 Silver equals 1 Gold.

Prices may be written using the full denomination or the abbreviations C, S, and G. Listed prices represent ordinary goods in a place where the item is reasonably available. Scarcity, local materials, law, war, and distance may change a price or make an item unavailable.

## Carrying Coins

Coins are **B(100)** and are Like-Items. Combine all Copper, Silver, and Gold coins before calculating their EP: any assortment of up to 100 coins is 1 EP, 101–200 coins is 2 EP, and so on.

Jewelry and other small valuables can also be treated as Like-Items when appropriate. A silver ring and a jeweled gold ring do not each begin a separate bundle merely because their materials differ.
