### [Exotic Weapons](/data/weapon-groups/exotic-weapons/)

Exotic Weapons do not share proficiency with one another. Each weapon's proficiency must be purchased separately, even though they are collected in one table for convenience.

Cross-Training relationships for Exotic Weapons are listed in the [Weapon Proficiency Level rules](/playersguide/02-character_creation/04-math/#cross-training). This does not cause them to share proficiency.
