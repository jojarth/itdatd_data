        }))
);

Each weapon has a list of properties and features. Weapon properties are always applied to a weapon, regardless of your proficiency level with the weapon. 

Weapon Features require you to be of the appropriate Proficiency Level to utilize them. For example, if you are an Expert with a weapon group, you can utilize all Weapon Features in the Proficient, Skilled, and Expert columns of the weapon you are wielding.

Weapons in the Exotic Weapons category do not share proficiency. Each Exotic Weapon must be improved separately.

## Weapon Properties

Weapon properties provide a base on how a weapon functions. Weapon properties are applied regardless of your proficiency level with the weapon.
