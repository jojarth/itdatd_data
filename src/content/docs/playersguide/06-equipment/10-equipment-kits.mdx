Equipment kits are prepared shopping lists. Buying a kit does not discount its price, change the EP of its contents, or allow its contents to share a special container. Once purchased, record and use every included item normally.

## Component Kit

A Component Kit costs 10 Silver and contributes 2 EP. It holds ordinary, inexpensive spell components and is governed by the material-component rules in Chapter 9: Spellcasting.

## Regional Arms and Armor Kits

These packages are common regional loadouts. Their EP assumes that armor is donned and shields are wielded.

### Warriors of Aslund

Aslund warriors commonly favor medium armor and practical one-handed weapons. Animal hides protect exposed areas during the region's cold winters, while axes remain popular because they are inexpensive and useful as tools.

<div class="powder-blue-table compact-table">

| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |

| Militia | Battle axe, dagger, medium armor, medium wood shield | 72 Silver | 6 |
| Wealthy | War sword, hatchet, medium armor, medium wood shield | 91 Silver | 6 |

</div>

### Warriors of Oraktos

Oraktos warriors commonly favor spears and shields. The region's hot summers and wet winters make heavy armor uncomfortable and difficult to maintain.
