Armor protects a character passively while it is properly donned. You can don only one suit of armor at a time; all other armor uses its Packed EP.

## Reading the Armor Table

- **AC** is the Armor Class established by the armor before other adjustments.
- **EP Equipped/Packed** gives the armor's encumbrance while donned and while carried as cargo.
- **Cost** is the ordinary purchase price where that armor is available.
