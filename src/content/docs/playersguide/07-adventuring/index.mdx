Adventuring is everything that happens between the decision to leave safety and the moment you return to it. Combat is only one part of an expedition. Most of the danger comes from darkness, distance, hunger, exhaustion, and the slow drain of time.

## Using This Chapter

The pages in this chapter cover:

- **Time and Scale**, below, which sets the units of time used throughout play.
- **Exploration**, the turn-by-turn procedure for delving into dangerous places.
- **Wilderness Travel**, for journeys measured in days.
- **Rest and Recovery**, which defines the Night's Rest and the Safe Rest.
- **Lasting Injuries**, covering broken bones and other wounds that linger, and how they heal.
- **Provisions and Survival**, covering food, water, and going without.
- **Afflictions and Conditions**, covering poisons, diseases, curses, and the conditions they cause.
- **Hirelings and Henchmen**, including Loyalty and Morale.
- **Establishments**, for characters who build or hold a place of their own.
- **Downtime**, for the weeks spent between adventures.
