## Redo Me:
>There is no limit to the number of establishments a character may own or oversee, provided they meet the requirements for each.
>
>However, each establishment introduces additional followers, resources, income, expenses, and other responsibilities. Managing multiple establishments can add a considerable amount of bookkeeping and may slow the pace of play.
>
>For most campaigns, we recommend beginning with a single establishment. If you enjoy domain play and wish to expand your holdings later, you can always establish additional domains as your influence grows.
