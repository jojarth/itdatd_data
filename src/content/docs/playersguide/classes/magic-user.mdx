# Magic-User

Magic-Users are characters who specialize in the casting of magical spells. Playing a Magic-User gives you access to more magical power than any other class.

## Magic-User Gameplay

<GameplayCard
  type="class"
  eyebrow="Class Gameplay"
  title="Magic-User Gameplay"
  subtitle="A fragile scholar whose strength comes from preparation, spell selection, and careful positioning."
>
  <FeatureSection title="At the Table">
    Magic-Users are strongest when they can prepare for a problem before it becomes a crisis. Their spells can bypass obstacles, control danger, and reshape an encounter, but they are not built to survive prolonged punishment.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Pressure Points">
    Keep track of light, distance, memorized spells, and the cost of spending magical resources too early. A Magic-User who survives long enough to choose the right moment can change the shape of an expedition.
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>
