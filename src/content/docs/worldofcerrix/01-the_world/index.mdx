# Chapter 01: The World

Cerrix is a world of several great continents, wide oceans, and vast spaces hidden beneath the ground. This chapter describes what the world is made of and gives a brief look at its continents. For now, this book concerns itself mostly with **Enolin**, the continent where most campaigns take place (see *Chapter 02: Enolin*).

## The Composition of the World

At first glance, Cerrix is built much like our own world. A rocky crust and upper mantle, the **lithosphere**, rests on a hotter, softer layer below it, the **asthenosphere**. Unlike our world, though, Cerrix has a third layer between the two, which scholars call the **pneumosphere**.

### The Pneumosphere

The pneumosphere is made up of serpentinized peridotite, hydrated olivine, and many carbon-rich lenses of melted rock, but most of it is volatile gas: water vapor, carbon dioxide, and oxygen.

No one on Cerrix has discovered the pneumosphere itself. Its effects, however, are felt across the world.

### The Underworld

Deep within the lithosphere lie massive caverns where people and creatures can live. Many are the size of very large islands, and a few are as large as continents. On our world, caverns like these would be impossible: the pressure, the heat, and the lack of breathable air would see to that. **[DRAFT]** On Cerrix, the pneumosphere makes them possible.

## The Continents

Cerrix is made up of several great continents. The ones known to the people of Enolin are described below.

### Enolin

Enolin is a large and diverse continent, and the main campaign area for Cerrix. Its countries draw their inspiration from many European nations. Enolin is described in full in *Chapter 02: Enolin*.

### The Great Expanse

Far to the west, across the great ocean, lies the Great Expanse. Many tribes make their homes there. The largest of them is the Anahwaan, and outsiders tend to group all the peoples of the Great Expanse under that name.

### Bambesa

South of Enolin lies the great continent of Bambesa, a land of deserts, savannahs, and forests. At its heart lies Cassamba, a great savannah in central Bambesa.
