:::note[Inspiration]
Aslund draws on Norway, Denmark, and Iceland. Deinland draws on Germany and Denmark.
:::

## Aslund

Aslund lies far to the north, a land of fjords, mountains, and tundra. Its people, the **Aslundians**, are known throughout Enolin, and feared by many of its other countries. They are brutal warriors, expert survivalists, and unparalleled sailors. Those who get to know them, however, find that the Aslundians are also progressive in many of their ideals.

### Classes of Aslund

The following classes are native to Aslund. They follow all the rules for classes in *Chapter 05: Classes* of the Player's Guide.

* [**Berserkir**](/worldofcerrix/02-enolin/01-aslund_and_deinland/berserkir/): warriors who enter the bear's fury.
* [**Jofurr**](/worldofcerrix/02-enolin/01-aslund_and_deinland/jofurr/): boar warriors who drive through shield walls.
* [**Skald**](/worldofcerrix/02-enolin/01-aslund_and_deinland/skald/): warrior-poets who stir the blood of their allies.
* [**Ulfhednar**](/worldofcerrix/02-enolin/01-aslund_and_deinland/ulfhednar/): wolf warriors who hunt as a pack.

## Deinland

Deinland was once part of Aslund. When wars of religion broke out in the south, Deinland broke away, and recent changes in both religion and trade have widened the split. Today, its people, the **Deins** or **Deinlanders**, are a people of their own.
