Jofurr are boar-warriors consecrated to the gods. They stand at the point of the battle wedge beneath helms crowned with the boar, and the holy power that wards them holds firm where other warriors would break.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Svinfylking"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Jofurr Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
As an activity costing 2 Actions, starting in Phase 2, you can enter a battle trance called the Svinfylking. You gain the <ConditionLink id="battle-trance" /> condition for as long as your Svinfylking lasts.

<FeatureSection title="Battle Trance Effects">
  * You can move through a space that a foe occupies, so long as you do not end your movement there.
  * When you hit a foe wielding a shield with a melee attack, it must make a Break Roll for its shield, as you batter its defenses aside. If it used Shield Block against the attack, increase its shield's Break Chance by 2.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Ending the Battle Trance">
  * When your Svinfylking ends for any reason, you have [2 fewer Actions](/playersguide/08-combat/#fewer-actions) each round until the end of the following round.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 9th Level">
    * At the start of each Phase, each ally adjacent to you gains the benefits of your Svinfylking until the end of that Phase.
  </FeatureSubsection>
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 13th Level">
    * When your Svinfylking ends, you have only 1 fewer Action instead of 2.
  </FeatureSubsection>
</FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Bristleback"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Jofurr Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, you can take blows meant for the warriors beside you. You can use your Shield Block Reaction when an ally adjacent to you is hit by an attack you can perceive, as long as you are wielding a shield with which you are proficient.

When you do, your Shield Block reduces the damage your ally takes instead of damage you take. Your shield makes its Break Roll as normal.
</GameplayCard>
