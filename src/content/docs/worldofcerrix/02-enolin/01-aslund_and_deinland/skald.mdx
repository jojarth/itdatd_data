Skalds are warrior-poets. They stand in the shield wall beside those whose deeds they sing, and their chants can stir allies to battle fury. When the fighting is done, it is the skald who decides how it will be remembered.

## Skald Gameplay
Below is a list of information needed to play a skald at the various levels of play.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Witness of Deeds"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Skald Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Those who fight where a skald can see them know their deeds will be remembered. Your henchmen and hirelings within 30 feet of you who can see or hear you gain a +2 Situation Bonus to Morale.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Stirring the Blood"
  subeyebrow="3rd Level Skald Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 3rd level, you can roar a war-verse that sets an ally's blood burning. As an activity costing 2 Actions, starting in Phase 2, choose one ally within 30 feet who can see and hear you. That ally gains the [Battle Trance](/playersguide/08-combat/#battle-trance) condition for as long as your Stirring the Blood lasts.

<FeatureSection title="Battle Trance Effects">
  * The ally reduces the Speed Factor of any melee weapon they wield by 1 (to a minimum of 2).
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Maintaining the Battle Trance">
  * You, not the ally, spend the Action to maintain the ally's Battle Trance. The ally must be within 30 feet of you and able to see and hear you when you do.
  * Stirring the Blood is not a Spellsong. You can weave its chant into your Galdr, maintaining it while you perform a Spellsong without ending either.
  * If you can't be heard, such as when you are struck mute or magically silenced, you can't start or maintain Stirring the Blood.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Ending the Battle Trance">
  * You, as well as the ally, can end your Stirring the Blood at any time (no Action required).
  * You can stir only one ally at a time. Stirring a new ally ends the Battle Trance on the first.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 9th Level">
    * You can stir up to two allies at a time. When you start Stirring the Blood, you can choose up to two allies, and a single Action maintains the Battle Trance for both of them.
    * If you stir a new ally while two allies are already stirred, choose which of them loses their Battle Trance.
  </FeatureSubsection>
</FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Climb Sheer Surfaces"
  subeyebrow="5th Level Skald Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 5th level, a Skald can climb upon surfaces that seemingly have no handholds. While other allies may be able to scale walls with good handholds or ropes, Skalds need no such things to be able to scale a wall. Skalds must be wearing light or no armor, and be lightly encumbered or less to perform this action.

Skalds who meet these conditions can climb any walls. They must follow all other rules for climbing, but are able to scale surfaces thought to be impossible to scale unless by magic.
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Galdr"
  subeyebrow="7th Level Skald Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 7th level, your verses become galdr, chanted spell-charms that carry over the din of battle. You can cast Spellsongs, following the rules in [*Spellsongs*, Chapter 9: Spellcasting](/playersguide/09-spellcasting/#spellsongs).

<FeatureSection title="Preparing Spellsongs">
  * When you prepare spells, you can prepare **Spellsong** in place of a specific spell, in a spell slot of any level you can cast.
  * When you cast a prepared Spellsong, choose any Spellsong on the skald spell list whose level is equal to or lower than the slot's level. A spell slot can't be used to cast a cantrip Spellsong.
  * Cantrip Spellsongs work the same way. Prepare **Spellsong** as one of your cantrips, then choose any cantrip Spellsong when you cast it.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Accompaniment">
  * You accompany a Spellsong by beating out its rhythm as you start or refresh it: clashing a weapon against your shield, pounding your armor with weapon or fist, or beating a war-drum.
  * You can accompany a Spellsong whenever you are wearing armor or holding a weapon, shield, or drum. It requires no free hands and costs no additional Actions.
</FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="spellcasting"
  title="Spellcasting"
  subeyebrow="7th Level Skald Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 7th level, you have sung enough deeds into memory that the words have begun to answer back. The old poems are not only remembered; they are heard, by the living and by whatever listens beyond them. Each spell is a verse you have hammered into memory. When you perform it, the words are spent and slip from your mind until you rehearse them again.

Your spells are **clerical spells**, and you use the **Free-Hand** casting method. You otherwise follow the general rules in [Chapter 09: Spellcasting](/playersguide/09-spellcasting/).

  <FeatureSection title="Spell Preparation">
  As part of a Safe Rest, you may rehearse your spells. During this rehearsal, you recite the old verses aloud, beat out their rhythm, and fix each one firmly in memory.

  You may prepare any spell on the Skald spell list for which you have a spell slot of the appropriate level. The spells you prepare take effect when you complete the Safe Rest. If the Safe Rest is not completed, the spells are not prepared.

    <FeatureSubsection title="Spellsongs">
    You can prepare **Spellsong** in any spell slot and choose the song when you cast it (see *Galdr*, 7th Level Skald Feature).
    </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Spell Scrolls">
  You can cast spells from Spell Scrolls only if the spell appears on the Skald spell list.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Reversible Spells">
  Each time you prepare a reversible spell, choose whether you prepare its primary or reverse form. The chosen form is treated as the prepared spell.

  Preparing one form does not prepare the other. To have both forms available, you must prepare each separately.
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Casting Spells">
  You use the Free-Hand casting method described in [Chapter 09: Spellcasting](/playersguide/09-spellcasting/). Spellsongs don't require a Casting Hand (see *Spellsongs* in Chapter 9: Spellcasting).
  </FeatureSection>

  <FeatureSection title="Cantrips">
  Cantrips are war-cries and short verses, easily called to mind.

  When you prepare your spells as part of a Safe Rest, you may also prepare the number of cantrips shown on your Spell Progression table from the Skald cantrip list.

  Your Spell Progression table also shows the number of cantrip casts in your shared pool. You regain all expended cantrip casts when you complete a Night's Rest.

    <FeatureSubsection title="Spellsongs">
    You can prepare **Spellsong** as one of your cantrips and choose any cantrip Spellsong when you cast it (see *Galdr*, 7th Level Skald Feature).
    </FeatureSubsection>
  </FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>
