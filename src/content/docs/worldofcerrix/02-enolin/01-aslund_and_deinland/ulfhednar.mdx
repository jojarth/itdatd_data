Ulfhednar are wolf-warriors who hunt in packs. Clad in the skins of wolves, they raid and skirmish, strike from ambush, and run down their enemies with a wolf's speed and cunning.

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Pack Fury"
  subeyebrow="1st Level Ulfhednar Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
As an activity costing 2 Actions, starting in Phase 2, you can enter a Pack Fury. You gain the <ConditionLink id="battle-trance" /> condition for as long as your Pack Fury lasts.

<FeatureSection title="Your Pack">
  * When you enter your Pack Fury, choose one ally within 30 feet who can see or hear you. That ally becomes your **Packmate** until you choose a new one, it moves more than 60 feet away from you, or your Pack Fury ends.
  * Once each round (no Action required), you can choose a new Packmate the same way.
  * You and your Packmate are your **pack**.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Battle Trance Effects">
  * You and your Packmate increase your Move Speed by 5 feet.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Ending the Battle Trance">
  * When your Pack Fury ends for any reason, you have [2 fewer Actions](/playersguide/08-combat/#fewer-actions) each round until the end of the following round.
</FeatureSection>

<FeatureSection title="Feature Improvements">
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 5th Level">
    * When a member of your pack makes a melee weapon attack against a foe that another member of your pack is adjacent to, increase the attack's damage dice by 1 dice step.
  </FeatureSubsection>
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 9th Level">
    * When a member of your pack holds Combat Supremacy against a foe, every member of your pack holds Combat Supremacy against that foe.
  </FeatureSubsection>
  <FeatureSubsection title="When you Reach 13th Level">
    * You can have two Packmates at a time. Whenever you choose a Packmate, you can choose up to two allies instead of one.
    * When your Pack Fury ends, you have only 1 fewer Action instead of 2.
  </FeatureSubsection>
</FeatureSection>
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Tracker"
  subeyebrow="6th Level Ulfhednar Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 6th level, an Ulfhednar gains bonuses to track quarry and avoid being tracked. Increase your chance to successfully track creatures by +1-in-Y. If the GM instead calls for an Attribute Test, you gain a +2 Situation Bonus to the attribute being tested. 

You also know how to cover your trail. Any creature attempting to track you or your party increases the Y dice of its X-in-Y test by 1 dice step. If the GM instead calls for an Attribute Test, the creature has a -2 Situation Penalty on its test. 
</GameplayCard>

<GameplayCard
  colorSet="fixedfeature"
  title="Move in Utter Silence"
  subeyebrow="7th Level Ulfhednar Feature"
  headerOrder={['title', 'subeyebrow']}
  toc
>
Upon reaching 7th level, while attempting to be stealthy, an Ulfhednar can move so utterly silently that they make no noise. While other allies can be stealthy and quiet, Ulfhednar make absolutely no perceptible noises. Ulfhednar must be lightly encumbered or less to perform this action, and must be wearing light or no armor.

Ulfhednar who meet these conditions can stealth around while making absolutely no noise, and cannot be discovered by means of noise detection.
</GameplayCard>
