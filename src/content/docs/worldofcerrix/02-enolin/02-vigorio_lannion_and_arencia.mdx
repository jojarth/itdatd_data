:::note[Inspiration]
Vigorio draws on Italy, Lannion on France, and Arencia on Spain.
:::

## Vigorio

South of Deinland lies Vigorio, a robust land with an even climate. Its summers are hot and its winters mild, and rain in the spring and fall makes for long, generous growing seasons. Its people, the **Vigorians**, are known for their great arts and culture.

## Lannion

Lannion lies west of Deinland. Its natural borders make it a difficult country to attack. Its people, the **Lannions**, are known for their winemaking and their other culinary arts.

## Arencia

Southwest of Lannion lies Arencia, known for its beautiful coasts and mountains. Its people, the **Arencians**, are known for their great drive and passion.
