:::note[Inspiration]
Kennymare draws on Ireland, Gantham on England, and Mooreland on Scotland.
:::

Three major islands lie to the north of Enolin.

## Kennymare

Kennymare is home to the **Kens**, a rough-and-tumble people who brave the coastal mountains and marshy lowlands of their island.

## Gantham

Gantham is home to the **Ganthamites**, who are known for their industriousness, their intelligence, and their brutal politics.

## Mooreland

Mooreland is home to the **Moors**. Their island is a diverse one, with high mountains, many lakes, fertile farmland, and thick woodlands.
