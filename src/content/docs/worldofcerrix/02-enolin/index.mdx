# Chapter 02: Enolin

Enolin is the main campaign area for Cerrix. It is a large continent, and a diverse one. Its main countries are each made up of smaller principalities and states, and each draws its inspiration from one or more real-world European nations.

## The Countries of Enolin

The rest of this chapter describes Enolin's countries, grouped by region:

* **Aslund and Deinland**, in the far north.
* **Vigorio, Lannion, and Arencia**, south and west of Deinland.
* **Oraktos**, east of Vigorio.
* **The Northern Isles** of Kennymare, Gantham, and Mooreland.
* **Mahased**.
