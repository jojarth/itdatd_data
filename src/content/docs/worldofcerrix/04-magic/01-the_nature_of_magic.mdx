## The Nature of Magic

This section explains where magic truly comes from and how it works. It is written for Game Masters and world builders, not for players, and no campaign should ever need this much detail at the table. Instead, it gives you a foundation for deciding where powers come from.

This explanation of magic is not tied to any one time period. The same rules can support a campaign set in a medieval world, a campaign set among the stars, or one that moves between the two. For how the peoples of Cerrix understand and practice magic, see *Magic in Cerrix* later in this chapter.

### The Truth That No One Knows

Magic is part of the wider universe. The spellcasters who use it think they understand how it works, but they do not. Their studies have taken them down several different paths of manipulating energy, and every one of those paths has led to the same result: the ability to cast spells.

This cosmology should never be discovered in play. It should always remain a mystery. Every world should have an explanation for how magic exists, but that explanation should never be correct. This section is the true answer, and it exists only to help you write the wrong ones.

### The Universe

Every form of magic works the same way. It tears into the fabric of the universe, pulls "data" out of it, rearranges that data, and puts it back.

The universe is made of seven **domains**. In order, they are **Matter**, **Energy**, **Mind**, **Spirit**, **Time**, **Entropy**, and **Infinity**.

All seven domains are metaphysical. The Matter domain is the lowest of them, and it is where all life functions. Every other domain influences the Matter domain.

#### The Seven Domains

**Matter.** The Matter domain holds all physical matter, including our bodies. Some call it the physical domain. Many of the higher domains have quasi-physical ties to it.

**Energy.** The Energy domain is the physical manifestation of non-matter particles within the Matter domain. Although it is considered a separate domain, it shares physical space with the Matter domain. Plasma, the fourth state of matter, functions from the Energy domain, as do light, sound, heat, and other waves.

**Mind.** The Mind domain is where all conscious life lives. A living creature's consciousness is tied to a mass in the Matter domain. When a creature dies, its thoughts are absorbed into the Collective Unconscious.

**Spirit.** The Spirit domain is where all spirits reside. A spirit is what ties a consciousness in the Mind domain to a physical body in the Matter domain. A spirit not tied to a sentient creature drifts through the Spirit domain aimlessly and endlessly.

**Time.** The Time domain allows the Collective Unconscious to determine where and when to meld a mind with a spirit, tying it to the Matter domain.

**Entropy.** The Entropy domain keeps the Time domain from ever being manipulated. By constantly filling the Time domain with random errors, it ensures that there is never one true path, and any attempt to manipulate time is rejected. Entropy also has a direct tie to the Energy domain.

**Infinity.** The Infinity domain is a mystery. Some say it is where higher beings reside, and others say it is a higher being itself. The Infinity domain exists to regulate all the other domains, and its presence is what allows mortals to manifest magic as a power.

### Sentient Creatures

Matter can exist without a mind or a spirit, but a mind or spirit cannot exist without matter. Every sentient creature is a combination of mind, spirit, and matter.

All of a creature's thoughts exist in the Mind domain. Thoughts unfettered by a spirit join the **Collective Unconscious**, where all free minds drift, waiting for a spirit to whisk them away and tie them to matter.

When a sentient creature is born or created, a free spirit from the Spirit domain collects a mind from the Collective Unconscious and binds itself to that creature. The mind is removed from the Collective Unconscious and tied to the creature, and the spirit gives it sentience. Once the spirit no longer inhabits the creature, the mind is reabsorbed into the Collective Unconscious.

A mind keeps every experience it has ever had in its memory, but a spirit cannot cleanly access those memories. Between this, the Time domain's infinite causalities, and the chaos of the Entropy domain, the most a sentient creature ever glimpses of a past life is a moment of déjà vu.

#### Resurrection

The same rules make true resurrection nearly impossible. To bring a creature back, you would need its same body, its same mind, its same spirit, and its same place in infinite time, and Entropy would have to not interfere.

If you want resurrection in your campaign, don't make it a single spell. Make it a combination of spells cast by several different spellcasters, such as:

1. **[DRAFT]** A high-level magical spell that collects the creature's mind from the Collective Unconscious.
2. **[DRAFT]** A high-level magical spell that either halts Entropy's influence or finds a moment when its chaos has subsided enough for the resurrection to take place.
3. **[DRAFT]** A high-level clerical spell that locates the creature's correct place in time.
4. **[DRAFT]** A high-level clerical spell that locates the creature's spirit.
5. **[DRAFT]** A high-level clerical spell that binds it all together.

### Teleportation

Teleportation magic is exceedingly dangerous unless it is done through a portal. Because of the way magic interacts with the Matter domain, a spellcaster cannot accurately teleport: the world is rotating, it is circling its star, and it is hurtling through space.

On a world like ours, in a single second, a spellcaster would be off by a little more than a quarter of a mile because of the world's rotation, by eighteen and a half miles because of its orbit, and by almost 143 miles because of its movement through space. In a campaign with advanced technology, computers might help make these calculations. Without them, accurate teleportation is nearly impossible.

Instead, teleportation is done through **portals**. A portal is a grounded location that a creature can teleport to. Because the portal is already moving with the world, the traveler arrives at the exact right spot in space and time as the magic takes effect.

### How Magic Interacts with the Domains

With rigorous training, a spellcaster can gain entry to the domains through their magical mind. How they gain entry affects how they manipulate the data of the universe, and which domains they can reach. Three things shape this: the spellcaster's spellcasting type, their class, and their level.

#### Spellcasting Type

A spellcaster's spellcasting type has the greatest effect on how they interact with the domains. The spellcasting type is the caster's **entry point** into the domains, and each type has strong access to certain domains, which shapes the kinds of spells it casts. Every spellcasting type can reach every domain, however.

##### Magical Spells

Magical spells reflect the arcane and eldritch work of learning to find and manipulate the data needed to make a spell manifest in the Matter domain. Magical spellcasting relies on pattern recognition. Its entry point is the Entropy domain, and it has the most profound effect on the Entropy, Energy, Matter, and Mind domains.

##### Clerical Spells

Clerical spellcasters seek to understand the order of the universe and their role in it. This soul-searching requires the deepest insight. Most people pursue that understanding through religion, but clerical spellcasting is not strictly tied to religion.

Clerical spellcasting's entry point is unique: it enters through the Infinity domain. Even so, it has the most profound effect on the fewest domains of any spellcasting type: only the Matter, Spirit, and Mind domains.

#### Class and Level

Editing the data of the universe is difficult. A spellcaster's class and level determine how much of it they can influence, in the form of their spell slots and the highest level of spell they can cast. These start small, because a 1st-level character is only beginning their spellcasting journey, and they grow as the character gains levels.

### Travel to Other Domains

Unlike in many other roleplaying games, travel to the other domains is impossible. There will never be an adventure in which characters travel to any domain other than the Matter domain.
