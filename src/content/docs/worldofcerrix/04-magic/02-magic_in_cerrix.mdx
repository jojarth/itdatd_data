## Magic in Cerrix

This section describes how magic came to the peoples of Cerrix and how they practice it today. The true nature of magic, which no one in Cerrix knows, is described in *The Nature of Magic* earlier in this chapter.

### Magical Spells

The study of magic began with the elves, who were the first to "see" the data of the universe. They simply call it **the Arcane**. As the elves developed ways to tap into the Arcane and cast spells, it became a focus of elven society. In time, that knowledge passed to half-elves, and from them to other peoples.

**[DRAFT]** Elves learn magic at an early age, and many elves blend it with martial training before they venture into the wider world.

Dwarves cannot harness the Arcane. Their strict and lawful essence is anathema to Entropy, the domain through which magical spells enter the world. No dwarf can become a magical spellcaster, and dwarves are highly resistant to the effects of magical spells.

### Clerical Spells

Clerical spells are rooted in deeply religious practice, because religion is the most common path to the insight that clerical spellcasting requires. In the end, clerical spellcasters fall into two broad traditions: **orthodoxy**, right belief, and **orthopraxy**, right practice.

* **Orthodox** spellcasters improve themselves through doctrine, seeking to bring themselves closer to the ideal laid out by their god or gods. They are most often found in organized religions, such as the churches of the Patristic Faith, where adherence to doctrine is what turns a spellcaster's gaze inward.
* **Orthopraxic** spellcasters hold themselves to a specific conduct, often one that relies on older rituals. They are most often found in unorganized religions, such as the Old Ways, where the practices and ethics themselves are what turn a spellcaster's gaze inward.

**[DRAFT]** Strict orthodoxy is anathema to how elves live their lives, so elves rarely become orthodox spellcasters. Many instead turn to orthopraxy, and for many elves who study the Arcane, it is highly likely that they take up its practices later in life.

### Resurrection

True resurrection does not exist in Cerrix. The dead do not come back.
